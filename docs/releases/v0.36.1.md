# Harbor City 0.36.1: the original people, walking naturally

- **The original Harbor City people are back** (the animated Kenney people from 0.35 are switched off; they stay in the project).
- **More natural walk**: legs swing from the hip with the knee lifting through the step and a soft bend on landing, relaxed arms swing opposite the legs a little away from the body, the body bobs and sways and the head stays level.
- **Living idle**: people standing around breathe, slowly shift their weight, move their arms a little and look around now and then, each on their own timing.
- **No buried feet**: walkers, crowds, you and other players stand on raised pavements, plazas, Food Court paving and the Harbor Commons floors instead of sinking into them.
- **Other players walk**: online players now move their legs instead of gliding.
- **Online by default**: the game joins online play when it starts. Turn it off in Menu > Online and it stays off until you turn it back on.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (new check: 0 buried feet among standing crowds).
