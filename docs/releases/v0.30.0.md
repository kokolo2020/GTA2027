# Harbor City 0.30.0: a real-looking city

Phase 1: buildings and streets
- **Photo textures** on roads, pavements, lawns, walls and roofs: real asphalt with cracks, grey concrete pavements, grass, brick, stucco, stone, concrete, corrugated metal and clay roof tiles. Each building keeps its own colour.
- **Glass towers** have window frames and reflect the sky. At night they light up window by window (about 4 in 10, in warm and cool shades) instead of whole glowing walls.

Phase 2: trees and vehicles
- **Trees**: round, leafy crowns on a real trunk with branches. Pines have ragged layered tiers, and palms have a curved trunk with drooping fronds.
- **Cars**: all five are rebuilt with real shapes (sedan, estate, SUV, coupe, supercar). They have sloped windscreens, rounded bodies, door lines, mirrors and rims, and glossy paint. This includes your car, traffic and other players.
- **City bus**: a real bus with a window band, doors, a lit destination sign and a roof unit.
- **Harbor Loop sky train**: smooth, rounded carriages with a sloped nose and red tail lights.

The textures are free CC0 photo textures from Poly Haven (credited in credits.txt). The browser version skips their bump detail to stay fast. The app is bigger, about 40 MB, and the browser download is about 14 MB.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed. Desktop frame rate is about 66 fps, or 80 fps with the browser settings. Not yet tried on the Fold5 or in a real browser. People are being redesigned separately.
