# Chit Chat City 0.59.0 - Portal to Paris

- New portal gate on the plaza south of the Harbor Dome. Step in and you arrive in a real Paris district: the streets, river and about 590 buildings around the Eiffel Tower, taken from OpenStreetMap and drawn in the game's style (Haussmann-style fronts with windows and roofs, trees, lamps, a walk-under Eiffel Tower, the Seine and its quay).
- Café de la Tour: order a café in Paris.
- 4 daily Paris quests (step through the portal, stand under the tower, order at the café, look at the Seine from the quay): 300 pay and 30 XP each, 1,500 pay and 150 XP for all four. Progress is saved.
- A return portal in the Champ de Mars brings you home. Paris builds on first entry (about 0.2 s on a Mac).
- Credit: Paris map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Shown in the Menu credits line and credits.txt.

Validation (Mac): full test passed. Not yet checked on the Fold5 (frame rate and memory in Paris need a look on the device).
