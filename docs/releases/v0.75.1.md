# Bonkers City: Boss Night! 0.75.1 - JOIN banner

- Call to action for boss fights: when anyone wakes a boss, every other online player gets a big red banner at the top of the screen: "BOSS FIGHT in PARIS! ... JOIN". Tapping JOIN drops them next to the boss. The banner stays for as long as the fight is going, and X hides it.
- It no longer depends on one message getting through: the banner follows the fight's heartbeat, which is sent every 2 seconds, so players who come online in the middle of a fight see it too.
- The player who wakes the boss is told what happened: how many players were called, that nobody else is online, or that they are offline.
- Players already in the city see the boss wake immediately.

Validation (Mac): smoke test, portal-city test, self-test (banner shows on a call, goes away when the fight stops, comes back on the heartbeat, hides once you are in that city), real render. The original report was not reproduced, so this needs a check with two or three real devices.
