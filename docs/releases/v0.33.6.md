# Harbor City 0.33.6: Harbor Food Court

The old Mint Cruiser block in the old city is now the open-air **Harbor Food Court**:

- **Three hover stands** for fresh orange juice, hot dogs and smoothies. Each one is a pearl-white pod floating on a neon ring, with a glass counter, a hologram menu, a glowing halo canopy and a spinning hologram treat on top (an orange, a hot dog and a smoothie cup).
- **Robot vendors** with glowing visors serve behind each counter.
- **Busy crowds**: queues at every stand, people waiting around and people eating at neon tables. Someone in the crowd waves, claps or cheers every few seconds.
- A neon **HARBOR FOOD COURT** arch on the street, and the billboard now advertises the court.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed, including a check that the court and its crowd are there. About 50 fps on desktop standing right next to the court; the browser version shows a smaller crowd. Not yet tried on a phone.
