# Harbor City 0.34.3: streamer mode

Going live? Turn on **Menu > Streamer mode** (tap again to turn it off):

- A pulsing **● LIVE** badge with how many players are online. Tap it for the Menu.
- A big **Play with me!** card with the join QR code and web address, so viewers can scan it from your stream and join your city.
- **Today's leaders** across the top (top level and richest player), refreshed from the leaderboard every 45 seconds.
- A **clean screen**: the small QR code, QUESTS, PLAYERS, money and online pills and chat pop-ups are hidden; the joystick and big buttons stay faint so you can still play.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed, including turning streamer mode on and off. Not yet tried in a real stream.
