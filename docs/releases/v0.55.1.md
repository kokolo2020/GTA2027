# Harbor City 0.55.1 — World Biosphere safety and mobile performance

Continuous sloped bevels replace the narrow entrance ramps around the hub and all seven dome floors. Sealed dome walls now meet the tunnel doors without side gaps. An enclosed continent loop connects all seven entrance tunnels, so visitors can go around the campus without returning through the hub.

Pens are moved away from public paths and raised structures. Every exposed ramp, bridge and deck edge has solid guard collision. A safety net returns an on-foot or BOARD player to the nearest open path if they enter a pen or hold the stick without moving for over 1.5 seconds.

Flamingos stay in Coral Lagoon. Standing bird models remain on the ground or a supported perch; dedicated flight models use spread wings, tucked legs and banked flight. Scenery is moved or resized to keep visitor corridors clear. Feeding targets are spaced apart, including the elephants, instead of merging models into one spot; model-load checks reject placeholders. The original elephant GLB's short hip pivots are extended to join its torso while keeping its feet on the ground.

Only the current dome's detail and animal models are loaded. The previous interior is freed when switching domes. Inactive campus surfaces are culled while inside; foliage uses alpha scissor and small embedded textures, exposed balcony rails are opaque, and distant wildlife updates less often. Android now uses the existing mobile-lite rendering path rather than desktop normal maps and full-resolution rendering. The dome's mobile render target is limited to about one million pixels, and its shadows are disabled; outside settings are restored on exit.

Entry stays free: no tickets or gates. Existing spotted-species bits, passport pages, quests, photos, ranks, money and other progress remain valid. Install over the existing app; do not uninstall.

Validation and measured before/after results are recorded in `docs/world-biosphere/0.55.1/VERIFICATION.md`. Native Mac Web-lite measurements are a comparison, not a measured Galaxy Z Fold5 frame-rate claim. Physical phone/YouTube coexistence remains to be confirmed on the phone.
