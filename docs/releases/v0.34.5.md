# Harbor City 0.34.5: the city 100 years from now

Includes everything from 0.34.4 (built but not released on its own):

- **Futuristic roads**: smooth dark graphite panels with thin glowing seams, no cracks. At night the streets become a glowing neon grid.
- **No more traffic-light poles**: each crossing has a glowing strip set into the road at the stop line (green, amber, red), plus short glowing bollards on the corners. Traffic still stops for them.
- **Sleek buildings**: brick, plaster and concrete walls are now smooth pearl panels in soft pastel tints, with a glowing band at every floor; roofs are blue solar glass. Pavements are smooth light stone.
- **Light-blade lamps**: slim white pillars with a glowing seam and a floating light disc, no arms over the road.
- **Sky train**: slim round white columns with a glowing seam and neon rings, a thinner white guideway with a light strip, and glass lift tubes at the stations.
- **Parked car goes home**: a car left for 2 minutes drives itself home so it never blocks a street. Tap CALL CAR to bring it back.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed, including the parked car going home and coming back. About 52 fps on desktop.
