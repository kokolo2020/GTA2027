# Harbor City 0.50.0: city careers and your own home

- **Careers** (Menu > PLAY > Careers): start a shift and your car becomes the work vehicle, with jobs one after another.
  - **Taxi Driver** (any level, yellow Solar Cab): pick up passengers and drive them across town; quick rides tip.
  - **Paramedic** (level 8): reach the patient, treat them, then rush them to Harbor General before time runs out.
  - **Police Officer** (level 12, white with flashing roof lights): answer a call, then catch the suspect who runs.
  - **Firefighter** (level 16, red): drive to the fire, get out and stand close to hose it down.
  - Ranks: Rookie, Pro (5 jobs), Expert (15), Chief (30) and Legend (60). Higher ranks pay up to x2.1, and every job gives 25 XP.
- **Homes** (Menu > ME > My Home): buy one at its FOR SALE door. Garden Loft $55,000, Westpark Townhouse $68,000, Galleria Loft $85,000, Aurora Sky Home $160,000 and the Meridian Penthouse $320,000 (with a pool terrace). Inside: a furnished room with a big window over the harbour. Walk to a glowing spot to decorate it with 21 pieces (sofas, TVs, an aquarium, a grand piano, an arcade cabinet, beds, plants, art). Go home from the menu any time, or get directions.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (every career job start to finish, ranks, buying, entering, decorating and leaving a home). Not yet checked on a phone.
