# Harbor City 0.35.0: new people

- **Everyone is new**: the blocky people are replaced by smooth, properly modelled characters with real faces, hair and outfits (Kenney's CC0 Animated Characters, credited in game/models/people/LICENSE-Kenney.txt).
- **Natural animation**: people walk, run and jump with real skeletal animation, and idle in different ways: looking around, chatting, checking their phone or sipping a drink. They sit properly at Harbor Dome tables, and wave, talk and cheer when they emote.
- **Your character and other players** use the new body too, still tinted with your chosen shirt, pants and skin, and wearing your hats and Neon Market gear (visors, halos, jetpacks, wings, crowns).
- Animation is distance-based so busy areas stay smooth: nearby people animate every frame, people further away less often.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed. About 46 fps on desktop.
