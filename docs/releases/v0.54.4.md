# Harbor City 0.54.4: World Biosphere paths and wildlife layers

Fixed entry into the Biosphere hub and all seven domes: raised floor rims were stopping the player even with open gates. Gentle entrance slopes and beveled ice floes now allow walking, running and hoverboarding. The canopy lookout meets its bridge without a step. Entry remains free; the level 7 unlock stays.

Four distinct Phase 1 layouts replace repeated paths and stages:

- Africa: winding savanna trail, elevated safari bridge and shaded lookout.
- Asia: bamboo switchbacks, curved climb and a circular terrace around a rain vortex.
- South America: river trail, bent/branching canopy bridges and a living-tree fort.
- Antarctica: ice-floe trail, glacier spiral and aurora summit.

The conservatory look returns with original Blender planting, triangular glass framing, transparent perimeter walls and animated water/mist. There are 32 extra animals across raised rock/tree habitats, hanging branches, a penguin ice colony, cliff nests and upper flight zones. They reuse existing species so spotted animals and completed passport pages stay valid. Europe, North America and Oceania remain later-phase conservation previews with different route outlines; Ocean World is still reserved for Phase 3.

Install over your existing app to keep progress. Same package and signing certificate. Debug-signed Android test build and updated browser version.

Validation: guarded import, focused Biosphere checks and full city smoke, including actual player movement in both directions at walk/run/hoverboard speeds, upper-animal containment, quests, passport, feeding, photos and daily streaks. Desktop saves restored byte for byte. Mac Web-lite measurements: 56.2 FPS downtown versus the original 52.0 baseline; 158.1 FPS in the detailed Asia scene. Scene-specific desktop results; phone walkthrough feedback is still needed.
