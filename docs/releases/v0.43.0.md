# Harbor City 0.43.0: Harbor Album, the free Harbor Pass, Harbor Night and check-ins

- **Harbor Album** (Menu > Album & Pass): five pages (Biosphere Animals, Sky Garden Plants, Relics, Wardrobe, City Discoveries). Each full page pays $2,000 + 150 XP; the whole album pays $10,000 more.
- **Harbor Pass** (free): 4-week seasons, 30 tiers at 150 XP each from any XP you earn. Cash rewards plus items at tiers 5-30 (Sunglasses, Glow Kicks, Mohawk, Golden Harbor Key, Leather Jacket, Ancient Crown). Items you already own pay $1,000 instead. Rewards come round again next season.
- **Harbor Night**: every Saturday 6-10pm (your time): Sky Run prizes x3 and twice the Sky Garden mutation chances.
- **Check-in calendar**: $100 each day you play; any 5 days in a week (Monday to Sunday) pay $1,500 + 100 XP. Missing a day never resets your count.
- **Blender birds**: penguin, flamingo, snowy owl, macaw, mallard duck and Canada goose, with flapping wings.

Everything is earned by playing; nothing costs real money. Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.
