# Harbor City 0.25.1: FIND ME fixes and a smoother browser version

- **FIND ME counts anywhere on the glow**: a find now counts anywhere on the glowing floor (it needed you within 3 m of the exact centre, so the edge of the glow often missed). Your phone keeps reporting every 3 seconds until the hider confirms, so one lost message no longer costs you the find.
- **Finders see their place**: the top banner changes from "FIND Kokolo" to "Found Kokolo! Waiting for your place..." and then "You found Kokolo! 1st place +$200". The hider also repeats the winners list every 3 seconds, so a finder gets paid even if one message drops. Still paid only once.
- **Smoother browser version** (/play only; the Android app is unchanged): the 3D world draws at about 1.1 million pixels and is scaled up to the screen, with menus and text kept sharp, and shadows use one layer over 55 m instead of two over 110 m. On a desktop check at a large window this went from 67 to 88 fps. Add `?fps=1` to the play address to show a frame counter.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed:
- FIND ME banner states, repeat reports, paid once even from the announce
- all earlier checks

Not yet measured in a real browser or tested with real phones online together.
