# Harbor City 0.32.0: play together

- **Players & Friends**: while online, tap PLAYERS (under QUESTS, or in the Menu) to see everyone online by distance. For each player you can:
  - **+ FRIEND**: send a friend request. Friends get a ★ on their name tag.
  - **HIGH-FIVE**: within 12 m. Both of you get +10 XP.
  - **GIFT $100**: up to 10 gifts a day.
  - **INVITE**: they can accept to come to you.
  - **GO**: jump to a friend or crewmate.
- **Kindness**: gifts and high-fives raise your kindness (♥), shown on your name tag.
- **Pop-ups on the clean screen**: friend requests, invites, gifts and crew invites show there, and gifts come with a thanks button.
- **Crews**: create a crew with a name and one of six colours, invite players with CREW+, and wear the crew colour on your ring and name tag. Quests done with a crewmate nearby pay 1.5×, and the board shows the crews online now.
- **Dance floor** at Times Plaza: a flashing colour floor with speakers. Tap DANCE, people standing there dance along, and dancing with other players gives more XP.
- **Group photo** (Menu): a selfie camera with a clear screen and a cheer. It saves to Pictures/HarborCity on Android and downloads in the browser.
- **Together bonus**: +20 XP a minute for each friend or crewmate near you (up to 3).
- **Same ID every time**: online uses a permanent player ID, so friends still recognise you.
- **Fixed**: a player leaving mid-dance or mid-emote no longer causes an error.

Friends, your crew and kindness are saved on your own phone; there is no shared server. Everyone needs 0.32.0 to see requests and gifts.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed, including a social test with a simulated player. Not yet tried with real phones online together, and photo saving is not yet checked on a phone.
