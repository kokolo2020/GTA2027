# Bonkers City: Boss Night! 0.77.0 - Four monsters, the World Eater, and a level 40 glow

- The four city bosses now look and fight differently:
  - Paris, THE EMBER DRAGON: flapping wings, fire breath, meteor rain and the ground slam.
  - Tokyo, THE NEON SERPENT: a giant cobra with a neon hood. Venom strike, constrict and venom spit.
  - New York, THE STONE TITAN: a rock giant with lava cracks. Rock stomp, rockfall and earthquake.
  - Bangkok, THE RIVER KRAKEN: tentacles and glowing eyes. River cross, tentacle grab and tidal wave.
- Beat all four bosses in one day and THE WORLD EATER invades Harbor Dome: a huge armoured eye with four heads. It needs more than 10 players (with 11 or more every shot hits 20 times harder) and pays $250,000 plus a level.
- Reaching level 40 gives your character a golden glow: two spinning gold rings, a floating halo and rising sparkles. Only you see it for now.
- Test and screenshot runs no longer post to the online leaderboard.

Validation (Mac): parse checks, self-test (V72_CHECK OK), real renders of all five monsters and of the level 40 glow, an Ultimate fight test (11 players hit 20 times harder, 3 players do not, five attack patterns, win pays and saves once). Not checked on the Fold5 or with 11 real players.
