# Harbor City 0.25.0: Daily quests with real items

- **Daily quests, no more money glitch**: the 20 quick quests are now daily quests. Each one pays once per day, then disappears from the Quests list and the minimap until midnight (your phone's date). Before, you could pick the quest you had just finished from the list and get paid again and again.
- **Fixed pay per stop**: from $64 in the old city up to $217 for far-away stops. Where you start from no longer changes the pay.
- **All 20 done gives a $500 bonus**. Quick quests then pause until midnight, and Switch activity takes you to delivery jobs.
- **A real item at every stop**, on the pavement by the building, not in the road: coffee on a café table, a watch on a stand, a pot of flowers, parcels, a toolbox, a lost cat, a birthday cake, a kite, library books, seashells, ticket machines and more. When you pick one up it's gone until tomorrow.
- The Harbor Raceway and Harbor Dome quest rings moved off the road onto the pavement.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed:
- each quest pays once a day, and a finished one can't be picked or paid again
- the next quest is the nearest open one
- every quest ring and item is off the road
- the all-done bonus, and everything coming back on a new day
- all earlier checks

Not yet tested on the Fold5 or with real phones online together.
