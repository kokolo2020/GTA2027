# Harbor City 0.49.0: 40 levels

- **Levels now go up to 40.** Up to level 20 nothing changes (each level costs 50 XP more than the one before). From level 21 every step costs twice as much: 2,100 XP for level 21, 100 more each level, 4,000 XP for level 40 (71,450 XP in all).
- **New achievements**: Rising Star (level 25, Gold), City Icon (level 30, Legend) and Harbor Immortal (level 40, Legend).
- **Name tags** turn gold from level 20, pink from 30 and white at 40; the rainbow ring stays from level 20.
- Players with more than 12,550 XP move straight up to their new level when they update, with the level-up celebration.
