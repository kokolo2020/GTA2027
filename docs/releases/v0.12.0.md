# Harbor City 0.12.0 — Levels Update

- Player levels 1-5 (max for now). XP comes from blue token finds (20), discovery sites (25), first trial clears (40), missions (60) and the first 6 deliveries (10). Repeatable activities give money only.
- Levels are calculated from your existing progress, so current saves convert automatically. Nothing is lost.
- Level shows in the HUD, Menu, Journal and on online name tags.
- Online police vs thief roles (Menu > Chase) and offline Chase mode from 0.10/0.11 are included.
- Level 5 unlocks (coming in later updates): car showroom, avatar customization, new map extension and quests.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate. Debug-signed test build.
