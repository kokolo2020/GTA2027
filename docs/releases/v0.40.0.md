# Harbor City 0.40.0: new looks for everyone, and Harbor Galleria

- **Better-looking people**: hair, facial hair, clothes and accessories are now separate Blender pieces that move with the body. 3 builds, 8 skin tones, 6 eye colours, 10 hairstyles in 12 colours, 5 facial-hair styles, 11 tops, 5 bottoms, 5 shoes and 8 accessories. Everyone in the city wears a random everyday outfit, and online players see your full look.
- **Harbor Galleria** (Menu > Travel > Harbor Galleria): a bright, toy-coloured glass-roofed mall north of the city with Thread & Co. (clothes and shoes), Shear Bliss Salon (hair, colour, facial hair, build), Glint (accessories) and Relic & Curio. Stand in the glowing ring and tap SHOP: the camera turns to face you, trying on is free, colours are free, and each store has a 30%-off deal of the day.
- **Relics**: 16 rare curios from $2,000 to $120,000; six are in stock each day. Wear one and it floats beside you.
- **Wardrobe**: Menu > Wardrobe changes into anything you own, anywhere.
- **Featured Today** podiums and the **Sky Run**: bounce pads launch you up five floating platforms to a daily prize ($300 and 60 XP).
- **Fixes**: Biosphere gates stay shut without a day pass even when you walk right up to them, the gaps beside the dome tunnels are closed, cars can't be called into the Biosphere, and you can no longer end up walking through walls after leaving a car.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept (your old Style Shop colours carry over). Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (new checks: every wardrobe piece loads and hangs on a body node, try-on is free and buying keeps only what you paid for, out-of-stock relics can't be bought, your look round-trips online, every Sky Run stage is reachable with real physics, gates stop you at hoverboard speed, no gaps beside doorways). Desktop benchmark unchanged (55.8 fps). Not yet checked on a phone.
