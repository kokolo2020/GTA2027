# Chit Chat City 0.66.0 - Daily streak and daily goals

- Login streak: the first time you open the game each day you get cash and XP, growing up to day 7, with a $10,000 bonus every 7th day. Missing a day restarts the streak.
- Daily goals (Menu > PLAY > Daily goals): three goals a day from Seal an outbreak, Rescue 3 survivors, Finish 3 daily quests, Earn $10,000. Each pays cash; finishing all three pays +$10,000 and 500 XP.
- Goals and streak are saved.
- Look: less distance haze so far buildings keep their colour, a softer tone curve (less white-out), a thicker, darker Eiffel Tower, and richer Paris buildings.
