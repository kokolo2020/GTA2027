# Harbor City 0.42.0: realistic zoo animals and futuristic kiosks

- **New zoo animals** made in Blender (blender-src/make_animals.py): smooth, organic bodies with painted markings for the zebra (stripes, mane, dark hooves and muzzle), giraffe (patches and ossicones), polar bear, arctic fox, deer (spots and antlers), rabbit, moose (palm antlers), raccoon (mask and ringed tail) and beaver (paddle tail). Legs, head and tail still animate as they walk.
- **Waterfront kiosks**: the four old box kiosks are now futuristic glass pavilions with floating oval roofs, chrome columns and neon bands: Noodle Bar, Holo News, Smoothie Lab and Gadget Hub.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed. Not yet checked on a phone (each animal is about 6,000 triangles and hides beyond 90 m).
