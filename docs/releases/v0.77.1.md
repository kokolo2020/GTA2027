# Bonkers City: Boss Night! 0.77.1 - Blender bosses and a Cash log

- All five bosses are rebuilt as smooth, detailed models made in Blender:
  - THE EMBER DRAGON: horns, teeth, a jaw that opens, big wings with membranes, spikes down its back and a spade-tipped tail.
  - THE NEON SERPENT: a coiled body, a raised cobra with a glowing hood, eye markings, fangs and a forked tongue.
  - THE STONE TITAN: faceted rock, glowing lava cracks and chest crystal, mossy shoulders, a crown of crystals.
  - THE RIVER KRAKEN: a dome head with glowing eyes and a beak, and eight long tapering tentacles with suckers and glowing tips.
  - THE WORLD EATER: an armoured shell with a giant eye and eyelids, a crown of spikes and glowing cracks; its four heads use the dragon and serpent models.
- Menu > ME > Cash log: lists where your money came from (by game feature), counted from this version on.

Validation (Mac): parse checks, self-test (V72_CHECK OK, including the cash log), Ultimate fight test, and real renders of all five bosses. Not checked on the Fold5 or with 11 real players.
