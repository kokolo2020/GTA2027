# Harbor City 0.51.2: Photo mode, Restart chapters, tidier top of the screen

- **Photo mode**: tap the camera button under the minimap to hide everything on screen (buttons, joystick, minimap, QR code, quest and chapter bars, arrows, chat, feed, messages, quick chat, name tags and player rings), leaving only the city. Drag on the left half to walk and on the right half to look around. One tap anywhere, Back or Esc brings everything back. The city keeps running while you line up the shot.
- **Restart chapters**: Menu > PLAY > Chapters > Restart chapters (it asks first) takes you back to Chapter 1, step 1. Chapters you already finished celebrate again but don't pay twice.
- **Tidier top of the screen**: the chapter bar sits right under the quest bar, and the guide arrow sits under the chapter bar. Nothing overlaps.
- **Quick chat in the app only**: the four quick chat buttons show in the Android app, not in the browser version.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (Photo mode hides everything and brings it all back, walking and looking stay in Photo mode; restart pays nothing twice; no overlaps at the top; no quick chat in the browser build). Not yet checked on a phone.
