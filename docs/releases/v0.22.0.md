# Harbor City 0.22.0: Street-level camera, Call Car and new characters

- **New walking camera**: on foot, the camera now sits just behind your shoulder at street level instead of high in the sky. Drag the right side of the screen to look around. The camera swings in behind you as you walk, and moves closer when a building is in the way. The View button on foot switches between Follow, Far, Close and Eye.
- **Driving is unchanged**: it keeps its own view setting (Far, Close or In-car), and Far is still the default.
- **Call Car**: when your car is far away, the main button says CALL CAR. Your car honks and pulls up right next to you; tap ENTER CAR. There's an 8-second wait between calls, or 45 seconds as the thief in a chase. You can't call it into Harbor Dome or onto the race track; the raceway car park is fine.
- **Better characters**: everyone in the city has a proper face (eyes, eyebrows, nose, mouth, jaw), more realistic proportions, a collar, belt and shoes with soles. Knees and elbows bend when they walk, and their legs hang down when they sit. There are now 4 hairstyles: short, long, bun with ponytail, and curly.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed:

- Call Car wait time, and no calling into Harbor Dome or onto the track
