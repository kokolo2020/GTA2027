# Harbor City 0.34.2: Harbor Live and all your gear at once

- **Harbor Live**: the dark block building by West Arcade is now a futuristic plaza of giant screens.
  - Leaderboard screen: the live top 8, switching between Level, Money, Achievements and Kindness.
  - City News screen: players online, the next treasure hunt, the Party Quiz, the richest player and big achievements.
  - A ticker of announcements, a HARBOR LIVE sign, a spinning hologram trophy, neon floor rings and a watching crowd.
- **Wear all your gear**: wear every Neon Outfitters item you own at the same time. Tap a worn item to take it off, or TAKE ALL OFF. Players on 0.34.2 see everything you wear.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed, including wearing several items, other players' gear and both Harbor Live screens. About 52 fps on desktop.
