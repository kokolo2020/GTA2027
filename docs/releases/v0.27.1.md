# Harbor City 0.27.1: Traffic no longer gets stuck behind people

- **Fixed a traffic jam**: cars stop for anyone in front of them, but some of the people who stand around were placed in the road instead of on the pavement (two at the Harbor Waterfront and one by the Civic Center mall), so the cars waited forever. Anyone placed in a road is now moved to the nearest pavement.
- **Cars don't wait forever**: if someone stays in a car's lane for more than 4 seconds, the car drives on. Cars still stop for people crossing.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed:
- nobody stands in a road
- a car waits for a person in its lane, then moves on after a few seconds
- all earlier checks

Not yet tried on the Fold5 or in a real browser.
