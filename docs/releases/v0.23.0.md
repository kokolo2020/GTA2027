# Harbor City 0.23.0: RUN, Fast Travel, roadside job stops and taller trees

- **RUN**: on foot, tap the new **RUN** button above the stick to sprint (almost twice as fast). Tap it again to walk.
- **Fast Travel**: the new **Travel** button (under Chat) lists every district checkpoint. Tap one to jump there; your car comes with you. Only districts you have opened can be picked. There's a 20-second wait between jumps, and it's off during races, chases, time trials, bus rides and while seated.
- **Job stops on the pavement**: the golden pick-up and drop-off rings for delivery jobs are now beside the road instead of in the middle of the lanes and crossings. You can still trigger them from the car in the nearest lane.
- **Taller trees and street lamps**, so they look right from the new street-level camera.
- **Walking arrow moved to your feet**: in the Follow view, the yellow direction arrow now lies small on the ground in front of you instead of floating in the middle of the screen.
- **New hats**: the Cap, Top hat, Helmet and Crown are reshaped to fit the new heads. The cap no longer covers your eyes.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed:

- Fast Travel moves you and your car, follows district locks and the wait time
