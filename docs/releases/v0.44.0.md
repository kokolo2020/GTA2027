# Harbor City 0.44.0: Grand Central, 10 sky train stations, Harbor Gardens and people out walking

- **Grand Central** (across from Central Park): a big, busy plaza with the Harbor Ring (two giant rings turning over a reflecting pool), a glass hall with Cafe Orbit, Flower Bar, Gelato 21 and Harbor News kiosks, a terrace under umbrellas and a giant screen. Next door, two new towers stand on a street of 16 shops: Aurora Residences (homes) and The Meridian (offices).
- **Harbor Loop sky train, 10 stations**: new stops at Harbor Airport, Harbor Galleria, The Biosphere, Harbor Stadium, Grand Central and Civic Center, with three trains on the loop. Ride to every station in one day for a $1,200 City Tour bonus.
- **Directions**: Menu > Directions shows how far away every place is. Pick one and an arrow shows the way, with a chip at the top (tap it to stop). New signposts at the big junctions and stations point to the nearest places, with their distance.
- **The empty land is filled in**: Harbor Gardens, a park with a lake, fountain, lakeside cafe and trees between the Biosphere and the Galleria; rows of new lofts along Airport Road, beside the Galleria and west of the Biosphere; a stadium fan plaza with food trucks and team flags.
- **People walk around** the Galleria, the Biosphere, Grand Central, Harbor Gardens and the fan plaza: they stop at shop windows, go into shops, watch the animals and step aside for you.
