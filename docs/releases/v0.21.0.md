# Harbor City 0.21.0 — New Harbor Raceway circuit

- **A real racing circuit**: Harbor Raceway is rebuilt as a 1.42 km circuit with smooth curves. It has a long start/finish straight, a sweeping Turn 1, a back straight under a footbridge, a chicane, an infield hairpin and a tight final corner. Corners have red and white kerbs and gravel traps. Armco and tyre walls line the track, and there are two grandstands, a start-light gantry, a chequered line and painted grid boxes.
- **Pit lane and pit building**: 15 garages, race control tower, pit wall, pit entry and exit. Your race car now starts in the pit lane.
- **Park inside**: drive in through the gate to a big parking lot right next to the paddock, with about 240 bays. Bollards keep your own car off the track, and you walk a few steps to the PIT DESK.
- **Track racing only**: the long jump and drag strip are removed. You can race a Grand Prix vs robots, an Online Grand Prix or Time Attack. Boosters and spikes still work.
- **Fair AI**: the AI racers now drive exactly the same race car as you, with the same top speed (108 km/h), acceleration and booster and spike effects, and they get no catch-up boost. They only differ in how much they slow for corners.
- Old long-jump and drag records stay in your save, but they are no longer shown.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.
