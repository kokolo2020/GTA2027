# Harbor City 0.28.0: Downtown rebuilt big

The first step in rebuilding Harbor City at big-city scale. The Skyline District comes first; the other districts follow one at a time.

- **4-lane roads**: every downtown avenue and street is now 20 m wide, with a double yellow centre line, white lane lines, curbs, 5 m pavements and wide zebra crossings.
- **A bigger district**: Downtown is about 1.6 times wider on 80 m blocks, running from the North Highway out to the west coast. The airport road is now its north street, Westside Boulevard is 4 lanes too, and one avenue runs straight on into Westside.
- **Taller towers**: towers are about 1.7 times bigger. Harbor Tower stands about 165 m tall and City Hall about 100 m.
- **Bigger landmarks**: a 140 m Central Park, plus a bigger Harbor Stadium, Union Station and Grand Mall.
- **Everything moved to fit**: traffic now drives in proper lanes. Discovery sites, quests, checkpoints, people, the Skyline Dash trial and the Harbor Loop sky train were all moved to fit the new layout.
- **Fixed**: a sky-train pillar on a corner stood in a traffic lane. Pillars now stand only on straight stretches.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed:
- downtown entrances, lanes and buildings
- 33 traffic cars and more than 40 traffic lights
- sky train, airport, day and night
- all earlier checks

Desktop frame rate is about 70 fps, or 81 fps with the browser settings, about the same as 0.27.

Not yet tried on the Fold5 or in a real browser. The Old City, North Highway, Civic Center, Westside, Marina and Sunset Beach are still the old size.
