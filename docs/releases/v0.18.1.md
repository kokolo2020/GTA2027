# Harbor City 0.18.1 — Looking for players, people-only dome

- **Looking for players**: in Chat, tap "Looking for players!" to invite everyone online (once every 30 s, needs Online on). Invites appear in the new main chat with an ACCEPT button; accepting teleports you to the Harbor Dome entrance on foot, with your car parked outside. The inviter sees who accepted.
- **Main chat** at the bottom of the screen shows the last messages from online players.
- **Harbor Dome is people-only**: bollards at the end of the causeway stop cars but let people walk in, with parking right before them. A car found inside the dome is moved to the parking. The Harbor Dome checkpoint now sits next to the parking.
- Includes everything from 0.18.0.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (bollards stop cars but not people, car eviction, invite shows with ACCEPT, accept teleports, chat feed). Two-phone online play was confirmed working by Koko on 0.18.0; invites between two real phones are not yet tested.
