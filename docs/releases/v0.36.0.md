# Harbor City 0.36.0: Harbor Commons, daily puzzle and nearby chat

- **Harbor Commons**: the empty field by Bay Road is now a modern park, with a fountain plaza with dancing jets and a floating ring, benches with people resting, a playground (swings, slide tower, climbing dome, spinning disc and seesaw, with kids playing and parents watching), the Harbor Hoops basketball court with a player dribbling laps, trees, lights and an entrance arch. It is on the map.
- **Daily puzzle**: at the Puzzle Pavilion, tap DAILY PUZZLE. One puzzle a day, the same for everyone: Neon Lights (light all 16 tiles) or Slide 8 (put the tiles in order). The first solve each day pays $400 and 80 XP, plus up to $450 more for solving on days in a row.
- **Nearby chat everywhere**: a CHAT button under PLAYERS whenever you are online, with 20 phrases, 8 symbols and emotes. Your message shows as a speech bubble over your head for players within 40 m, and a chat log shows the area you are in, how many players are near and the last few lines. Switch "To: Nearby" to your crew to reach your crew anywhere; other players do not see crew chat. One message every 1.2 seconds.
- **Daytime by default**: the game starts in Always day. Saves that never chose a time switch to day once; Menu > Time still changes it.
- **Better poses**: legs no longer cross when walking, both feet point the same way, and arms hang relaxed a little away from the body with the fists facing the thighs.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (new checks for the park, the puzzle and nearby chat).
