# Harbor City 0.33.1: see who's online

- **Online count under the minimap**: even while you're offline, the minimap shows "● 4 ONLINE · JOIN" when others are playing, or "○ GO ONLINE" when nobody is. Tap it to go online with them. Once you're online it shows the full count, including you, and tapping it opens the Players list.
- **Staying hidden**: the count comes from quietly listening to the online lobby. You aren't shown to anyone until you go online.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed. A live check against the real online service counted a test player correctly. Not yet tried on a phone.
