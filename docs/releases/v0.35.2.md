# Harbor City 0.35.2: natural walking

- **New walk**: people land on the heel, bend the knee as they take their weight, push off from the toes and lift the knee through the swing. Hips sway, shoulders twist against them, relaxed arms swing opposite the legs and the head stays level. It replaces the slowed-down run that looked stiff and robotic.
- **Standing tall**: people stand up straight instead of crouching, breathe, and slowly shift their weight from foot to foot.
- Talking, waving, cheering, sipping a drink, checking a phone and sitting are redone on the new relaxed stance.
- Motion is made by blender-src/make_people_motion.py. Running and jumping are unchanged.
