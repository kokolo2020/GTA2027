# Harbor City 0.33.0: games, achievements and glory

Harbor Dome game centre
- **Party Quiz**: step into the glowing ring at the quiz stage. Everyone in the dome gets the same question at the same time: 8 questions a round, a new round about every 3 minutes. Faster right answers score more. The live scoreboard includes two robot contestants. The winner gets $300 and 50 XP.
- **Arcade**:
  - **Connect Four**: 4 tables, against the robot or player vs player. $10 to play, win $20.
  - **Reaction Duel**: tap first after GREEN. $5 a round, win $10.
  - **Memory Match**: 8 colour pairs against the clock, with a $20 bonus under 35 s and a best-times board.

Achievements
- **91 achievements** in Bronze ($100), Silver ($250), Gold ($750) and Legend ($2,000), with XP too. They cover levels, money, jobs, quests, exploring, treasure, trials, cars, chases, distance driven and walked, sky train and bus rides, every dome game, the quiz, racing, friends, gifts, high-fives, dancing and photos. See them all in Menu > Achievements, with progress.
- **Announcements**: Gold and Legend unlocks are announced to everyone online in the chat feed.
- **Already earned**: achievements you've already earned are awarded the first time you open this version.

Colours that show who you are
- **Ring colour = level**: green (1-4), cyan (5-9), blue (10-14), orange (15-19), rainbow at level 20.
- **Name tag and glow = best achievement tier**: bronze, silver, gold or purple for Legend. The tag shows your achievement count (◆).
- **Crew colour**: now a thin inner ring.

City and fixes
- **LED traffic signals**: sleek LED light-bar signals on slim white poles, with a new amber phase and a neon ring at the base.
- **Online at the edges**: players at the airport, far Westside or far Sunset Beach no longer vanish for others online.
- **Players & Friends panel**: it now closes properly.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed, with new checks for every arcade game, the quiz, achievements, announcements and highlight colours. Not yet tried with real phones online together.
