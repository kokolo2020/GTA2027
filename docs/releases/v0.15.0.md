# Harbor City 0.15.0 — Bus line, Marina stop, police helicopter and roadblocks

- Airport Line bus loops both ways between Marina Heights, Harbor Waterfront, Downtown, North Gate, Industrial Quarter, Maple Suburbs and Harbor Airfield. Tap BOARD BUS when it waits at a stop (fare $15) and GET OFF at any stop.
- The Marina Heights stop appears at Level 5. Until the North Gate opens the bus only runs Waterfront to North Gate.
- Thief chase now escalates: police roadblocks (about 12 s, 40 s and 68 s in) block the road ahead for 14 s, and a police helicopter patrols from 30 s to 52 s. If it stays overhead the Busted bar fills slowly, so outrun it.
- Includes 0.13 Marina Heights and 0.14 (real walking characters, Airport Line bus).

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate. Debug-signed test build.
