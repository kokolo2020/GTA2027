# Chit Chat City 0.72.0 - Cure gun, city bosses, Portal Terrace, fruit quests

- Leaderboard fixed: scores stopped updating for anyone above level 20 (the online table only accepted levels up to 20, so the whole update was refused). Levels above 20 now show correctly and money, achievements and kindness update again.
- Portal Terrace: the world portal moved to its own stone terrace on the north edge of the Ridge, with five gates (Paris, Tokyo, New York, Bangkok, Mystic Sky). Walk through a gate and you travel at once: no menu, no button. Coming home lands you on the terrace.
- Cure gun in every portal city: tap FIRE (or press F). Infected roam the streets; a hit turns them back into people. Brutes take 3 shots.
- New city quests every day: cure 10 infected, rescue 5 trapped people (cure their guards first), cure 2 brutes, beat the boss. They are in the quest list with the arrow.
- A boss in each city (The Iron Ghoul, The Neon Oni, The Times Titan, The River Giant). Wake it at the BOSS LAIR. It needs 3 players: with 3 or more in the city every shot hits 10 times harder. Red rings on the ground explode, it roars and sends runners, the sky turns red. 5 minutes to beat it. First win $25,000 and 2,500 XP, then $8,000 a day.
- Call to action: waking a boss, or tapping CALL PLAYERS in a city, puts a message with a JOIN button on every online player's screen. JOIN drops them next to the boss. The world list and the Harbor Live news screen show where a fight is on.
- Players can now see each other inside the portal cities and on the sky island (before, anyone in a portal city was invisible to others).
- Fruit Garden quests: 16 quests in a row (one per fruit, Fruit Salad, Speed Harvest, Golden Fruit Hunt), a fruit collection, $20,000 for finishing them all, then one daily harvest. Walk into the garden and follow the arrow to the glowing fruit.
- Daily goals pool grew: cure infected, pick fruit, beat a boss.
- The quest list in the cities hides finished quests so it stays clear of the joystick, and its rows now work with a finger tap.

Validation (Linux Godot 4.7.2, cloud): parse, the v0.72 self-test (leaderboard, fruit chain, terrace gates, cure gun, rescue, boss, call and JOIN in all four cities), full smoke test, real rendered screenshots. Not checked on the Fold5, and the 3-player boss fight has not been played with three real devices yet.
