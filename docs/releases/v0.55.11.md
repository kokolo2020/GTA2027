# Harbor City 0.55.11 - Open reserves in all seven domes

- Asia, South America, Antarctica, Europe, North America and Oceania now match Africa: no pens, animals roam big open habitats right beside a raised visitor platform.
- Every dome has its own pathway design: different arm routes, walkway widths, deck and pier shapes, and its own entrance gates (torii, vine, ice, stone, log, rock arch).
- Each dome has its own scenery theme and landmark (pagoda, kapok giant, iceberg, windmill, redwood, red monolith), with sky birds on perches.

Validation (Mac): see release log. No Fold5 was connected, so phone FPS is unverified for the larger reserves.
