# Harbor City 0.56.3 - Dome Quests

- New Dome Quests: ten daily quests that only exist inside the Harbor Dome (walk in, visit the giant tree, the comedy stage, the Challenge Corner and both snowmen, hang out 3 and 10 minutes, find gift boxes, solve the hourly challenge). Each pays once a day, finishing all ten pays a $3,000 + 300 XP bonus, and a daily-visit streak adds up to $700 to the first quest.
- 12 glowing gift boxes appear around the dome every day ($100 to $250 each, gold ones $500). A DOME QUESTS button shows while you are in the dome; tap a quest for the guide arrow.
- The hourly challenge now has its terminal (Challenge Corner) inside the Harbor Dome.
- The Harbor Dome now has a white geodesic glass frame with glowing joints and a warm interior glow when seen from outside.

Validation (Mac): focused and full smoke passed (including a new Dome Quests pay-once test); screenshots checked. Phone FPS with the new frame and snow is unverified.
