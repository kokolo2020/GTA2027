# Chit Chat City 0.73.1 - Spell casting

- Your character now casts its spells instead of just standing:
  - Cure bolt (FIRE): turns to face the target, the right arm punches straight out with a ball of light in the hand, the left hand steadies it; the bolt leaves from the hand.
  - CURE BLAST: both arms sweep up over the head while the light grows, the head tips back, then both arms slam down and the shockwave bursts from the ground.
- It works while walking or running (the casting arms are laid over the walk).
- Other players do not see your casting moves yet; that needs a small online change and comes next if wanted.

Validation (Mac): smoke test, portal-city test, self-test, real renders of the three poses. Not checked on the Fold5.
