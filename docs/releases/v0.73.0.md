# Chit Chat City 0.73.0 - CURE BLAST, dramatic bosses, simple boss screen

- CURE BLAST, a special attack in the portal cities. Every hit with the cure gun charges it (curing someone charges it more). When the BLAST button flashes, tap it (or press G): a shockwave cures every infected within 16 m of you, and the boss takes a hit worth 25 shots (1,000 damage with 3 players).
- The boss moved to open ground in every city: the widest clear space, with no buildings and no trees inside its circle. In Paris that is further from the portal; the quest arrow and JOIN take you there.
- A dramatic lair: scorched ground, glowing cracks, a ring of black spikes, embers, and the giant buried to its chest. When woken it climbs out of the earth while the ground shakes, a wall of flames closes the ring, the title flashes on screen and lightning strikes through the fight.
- Boss music: an original dark, driving loop made in code (no files added). It speeds up when the boss is ENRAGED below half health. The radio pauses during the fight.
- Two boss attacks that take turns: the ground slam (one big red ring under you) and meteor rain (five small rings, a fireball falls on each). It still roars and sends runners at 75, 50 and 25 percent.
- Minions: every 12 seconds (8 when enraged) the boss throws out a wave of small red minions that run at the players. They are weak (a light bite, one shot each, at most 10 at a time) and each one you shoot charges your BLAST.
- Simple screen in boss fights: only the stick, FIRE, BLAST, the boss health bar, your health and a LEAVE button. Everything else comes back when the fight ends (or when you open the menu).
- You can wake the boss from anywhere inside its circle.

Validation (Mac): smoke test, portal-city test, self-test (lair on clear ground in all four cities, both boss attacks seen, minion waves, blast, music loop renders without clipping, rescue, gates), real renders. The music has not been listened to by a person yet, and none of this has been checked on the Fold5 or in a real 3-player fight.
