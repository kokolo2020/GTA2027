# Harbor City 0.20.0 — Harbor Raceway

- **Harbor Raceway**: a huge new racing area (about 415 x 400 m) east of the North Highway. Follow the Industrial Quarter cross street east. Opens with the North Gate.
- **Park outside, race inside**: bollards stop cars at the entrance. Walk to the PIT DESK and get the race car; everyone drives the same car, so it's fair. Your own car waits in the parking.
- **Grand Prix** ($50): 2 laps of a ~1 km circuit against three AI racers. Prizes $300 / $150 / $75. Leaving the track slows you down.
- **Online Grand Prix** ($50): online players get a JOIN button for 10 s and start together; finish times are shared.
- **Time Attack** ($20, $100 per new best lap), **Long Jump** ($10, pays by distance), **Drag Strip** 360 m ($10, $40 under 13.5 s).
- **Boosters** ($50, +45% speed for 3 s) and **spike strips** ($40, slow anyone who hits them for 2 s). AI racers use them too. Records on the pit leaderboard.
- Also includes 0.19 (Civic Center, nicknames, dome zoom).

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (entrance and bollards, clear track, race car stats and return, shop, AI racers, boosters, spikes, off-track slowdown, 2-lap win payout, real-physics long jump of ~31 m, drag timing, joining an online race). Not yet tested on the Fold5 or with two real phones racing.
