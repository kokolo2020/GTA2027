# Harbor City 0.48.0: twice as fast in busy places, places that open as you grow

- **Faster**: in busy places the game now runs about twice as fast (desktop, browser settings: Grand Central 22 to 44 fps, downtown streets 25 to 47, the Galleria entrance 15 to 30, the Waterfront 41 to 69). Nothing was removed: cars only check the people and cars near them, cars out of sight skip physics, people far away animate less often and fade out sooner, the inside of the Galleria is only drawn when you're in it or at a door, and the Biosphere's dense planting is only drawn near the domes.
- **Places open as you level up**: Harbor Galleria opens at level 4, The Biosphere at level 7 and the Sky Garden at level 10. Until then each one is a fenced site with a sign, and it costs nothing to run. When you reach the level you get a message and it appears. Players already at those levels see no change.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (locks close and reopen, no fast travel to a locked place, traffic still waits for people near you). Not yet checked in a real browser or on a phone.
