# Harbor City 0.53.1: new city characters

New male and adult female characters across city NPCs and online avatars, with an athletic male build, a fuller female silhouette and futuristic outfits. Settings > Character cycles Male / Female / Classic and remembers your choice. Classic restores your full saved wardrobe; the new models use the tech outfit family.

Running playback follows actual ground speed with a smoother cadence and stops when blocked. Walking and hoverboard defaults are unchanged. Standing crowds use varied resting poses, and lighter crowd models reduce rendering work. Online avatars share body choice and outfit colors.

Install over the existing app to keep money, cars, homes, missions and other progress. Same package and signing identity; no save migration. Updated Android test build and browser version.

Validation: import, focused character/animation/palette/online-body/sitting/Settings checks, full desktop smoke test, real gameplay screenshots and Classic fallback. Desktop save restored byte for byte. Scene-specific M1 Web-lite benchmark: 50.7 FPS versus 45.9 Classic. Not tested on a phone.
