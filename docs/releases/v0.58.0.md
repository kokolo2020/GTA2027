# Chit Chat City 0.58.0 - Rain, station voice, a more natural look, chat typing on phones

- Chat: you can now type in chat on phones. A tap on the typing line opens the keyboard (touch never reached text boxes before; only the ready-made phrases worked). Same fix for the nickname and crew name boxes.
- Weather: random rain showers (about one 10-minute slot in four, 2 to 5 minutes each). The sky greys over, drops fall around you (never under a roof) and the streets turn wet and shiny. Everyone online sees the same weather. No rain sound. Switch it off in Menu > Settings > Weather.
- Harbor Loop sky train voice: "Welcome aboard", "Next station: ...", "Arriving at ..." and the station name when you get off (uses the device's built-in speech).
- A more natural look: film-style tone curve, warmer and stronger sunlight, cooler and softer fill light, calmer colours, darker shadows. Computers also get soft contact shadows; phones and browsers skip those for speed.
- World Biosphere: every corner where a dome walk crosses the loop is now closed with a rail, and the entry walk has rails, so players cannot step out onto the lawns. Path hedges are now leafy bushes with blossoms that match the trees, and the lamp posts are proper park lamps with a glowing lantern.
- Sky train: no more pillars standing in the road. Where the track runs over a road, a frame with one leg on the pavement each side and a beam across carries it.
- Harbor Dome: the wall is solid all the way up with a lid on top, so nobody leaves over the glass.
- Roastery café: brighter toy-style colours, chunkier rounded shapes, baristas stand with relaxed arms (hands at counter height) instead of arms stretched out. Gift boxes no longer appear inside the café.
- Stream Mode (auto run): walks only on sidewalks with a clear path (no more running into buildings), travels somewhere new at least every 5 minutes, and shows a clean screen like Photo mode.

Validation (Mac): full test passed. Not yet checked on the Fold5 (phone keyboard, speech voice and frame rate in rain need a look on the device).
