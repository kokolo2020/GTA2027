# Harbor City 0.24.1: Treasure hunts, FIND ME!, Quests list and name tags

- **Treasure hunt every 5 minutes**: a chest is hidden somewhere in the city, the same one for everyone online. No arrow, distance or map dot, only clues: first the district, then the exact landmark, and in the last 90 seconds a golden light beam. The chest only shows up close. Reach it before the timer runs out for $300 + 100 XP; if time runs out it's gone and a new one appears.
- **FIND ME!** (Quests → FIND ME!, needs Online): put up $350 in prizes and you're frozen for 2 minutes on a glowing floor. Everyone online gets a hint of the area. The first three to reach you win $200 / $100 / $50 (+60 XP). Unclaimed prize money comes back to you.
- **Quests list**: a Quests button (top left) lists the 8 nearest quick quests with distance and pay; tap one to go there. Quick quests nearby show as orange dots on the minimap.
- **Name tags**: your name and level float above your head (or your car).
- **Player rings**: you stand on a gold ring and other real players on a cyan ring, so players stand out from people walking around the city.
- **RUN button moved** up beside the QR code, away from the stick, so it isn't switched off by accident.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed:
- treasure hunt timing, clues, one reward per hunt
- FIND ME prizes, freeze, order of places, refunds and hunter payouts (simulated players)
- Quests list, name tag
- all earlier checks

Not yet tested on the Fold5 or with real phones online together.
