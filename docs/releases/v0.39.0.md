# Harbor City 0.39.0: four different Biosphere domes, natural trees and day passes

- **Every dome has its own layout**, in one garden style (a lush ground garden with round fenced habitats and a raised level with cafe tables and lookouts):
  - Winter, Glacier Mountain: a spiral ramp climbs an ice peak to a summit cafe 8 m up; snowy owls circle above; Penguin Bay, Polar Coast and Arctic Tundra round the far side.
  - Spring, Vortex Terrace: a ring terrace circles the rain vortex, with blossom trees growing through it, reached by a curving ramp; Blossom Meadow, Bunny Burrows and a glass Butterfly House you can walk into.
  - Summer, Safari Bridge: a bridge 5.5 m up past the giraffes and zebras to a round lookout; the Coral Lagoon and a golden Macaw Aviary dome.
  - Autumn, Canopy Terrace: a wide terrace with two giant maples growing through it, a spiral ramp down its middle and lookout balconies over the Beaver Pond and Goose Marsh.
- **Natural trees and plants**, made in Blender: oaks, maples, cherry blossoms, pines, acacias and a giant maple with leafy crowns, plus bushes, ferns, grass and reeds.
- **Fuller domes and grounds**: heavier frames and tinted glass on a stone base, planted glass tunnels with arches and lanterns, hedges, trees and flower lawns outside.
- **Tickets**: the hub is the ticket hall. A $50 day pass from the TICKETS desk opens the gates to all four domes until midnight. The way out of a dome is always open.
- **Cleaner signs**: small animal plaques at each fence, name boards over the doorways; animal facts now appear when you spot an animal.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.
