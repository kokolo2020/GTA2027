# Harbor City 0.52.0: careers with cut scenes, celebrations and achievements

Every career (Menu > PLAY > Careers) now runs as a clear job loop. A step bar at the top shows where you are (for example 1 Pick up → 2 Drive → 3 Drop off) with the distance and the timer. Short cut scenes (2-4 seconds) play at the key moments; tap anywhere to skip. Each job ends with a big JOB DONE celebration showing your pay, tip, XP and rank progress, and the next job comes by itself after 3 seconds.

- **Taxi Driver**: the passenger waves at the kerb with a name tag showing where they're going. Stop next to them and they get in the back; you see them in the rear-view mirror asking for their stop. The meter runs as you drive. Arrive on time for a good tip; bumps cost you some of it. They get out, wave and rate you.
- **Paramedic**: a bystander waves you over to the patient. The stretcher goes into the ambulance, lights and siren come on, and you race to Harbor General, where the patient is wheeled in through the doors. Arrive in time for a bonus.
- **Police Officer**: each job starts with a radio call ("Suspect in a red car heading north..."). Siren on, chase the car, and the suspect gets out with their hands up.
- **Firefighter**: follow the tall smoke column, get out by the fire and face it to spray the hose. Keep spraying until the fire meter is empty and the steam rises.
- **20 new achievements**: First Shift, Regular, Pro and Legend for each career, plus Five-Star Driver (tips on 10 fares in a row), Golden Hour (10 patients in time), Clean Arrests (10 catches) and Dousing Hero (25 fires). Progress shows in the Careers panel.
- **Pay**: taxi fares now come from the meter. A police arrest on shift pays through the career (it no longer also pays the Chase prize on top).

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (each career start to finish with the cut scenes skipped, achievements unlock once). Cut scenes checked with desktop screenshots. Not yet checked on a phone.
