# Harbor City 0.51.0: Chapters, quick chat chips, Food Court start

- **Chapters**: a guided story in eight chapters (Menu > PLAY > Chapters) that visits every district on the map, from the Harbor Waterfront to the Sky Garden. A chip under the top bar shows the next step and how far it is, and the guide arrow points there. Some steps ask for deliveries, cars, time trials, career jobs, a home or a level. Each finished chapter pays cash and XP. Progress is saved. Directions, careers, quests, trials and races still take over the arrow while they are running.
- **Quick chat chips**: four buttons always on screen, even on the clean screen and offline: SCAN AND JOIN THE GAME, JOIN THE FUN, HANG OUT FOR FUN and SUBSCRIBE & LIKE. One tap shows it in a bubble over your head and sends it to everyone online.
- **Food Court start**: new players start on the paving in front of the Harbor Food Court stands. Players with a saved checkpoint still resume there.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (chapters point the arrow, advance on arrival and pay out; Directions and quests win over the chapter arrow; four quick chat chips; spawn in front of the food court). Not yet checked on a phone.
