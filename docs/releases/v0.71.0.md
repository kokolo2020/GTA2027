# Chit Chat City 0.71.0 - Fruit Garden

- The island south of downtown (Marina Heights) is now a fruit garden: orchards and plantations of mango, coconut, orange, banana, strawberry, lemon, peach, apple, pineapple, dragon fruit, papaya, grapes and watermelon, with paths, fruit crates, signs and a flower ring around the fountain.
- The Harbor Motors Showroom, Style Shop and Paint Shop, the fountain, yacht club, boardwalk and lighthouse are all still there and work as before.
- The garden has no walls or collision, so walking and driving through it is unchanged. It is easiest to see in the Sky view.
