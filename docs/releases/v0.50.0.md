# Harbor City 0.50.0: city careers and homes you can buy

- **Careers** (Menu > Play > Careers): take a job and drive it across the city.
  - **Taxi Driver** (level 1): pick up passengers and drive them across town; faster rides tip more.
  - **Paramedic** (level 8): reach the patient, treat them, then rush them to Harbor General before time runs out.
  - **Police Officer** (level 12): answer calls across the city and catch the suspect who runs.
  - **Firefighter** (level 16): drive to the fire, get out and stand close to hose it down.
  - **Ranks:** Rookie, then Pro (5 jobs, pay x1.2), Expert (15 jobs, x1.45), Chief (30 jobs, x1.75) and Legend (60 jobs, x2.1). Every job also gives 25 XP towards level 40.
- **Homes** (Menu > Me > My Home): buy one at its front door, where you'll see the FOR SALE sign.
  - Garden Loft $55,000, Westpark Townhouse $68,000, Galleria Loft $85,000, Aurora Sky Home $160,000 and Meridian Penthouse $320,000 (with a pool terrace).
  - Each home has a furnished room with a big window over the harbour.
  - Walk to a glowing spot to decorate it. There are 21 pieces to buy, such as sofas, TVs, an aquarium, a grand piano, an arcade cabinet, beds, plants and art.
  - Go home from the menu, or get directions to any home.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.
