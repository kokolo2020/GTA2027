# Chit Chat City 0.67.0 - Infected zones

- Outbreak missions now play in a dead, infected city: red-brown sky, ash-grey ground, dry gold trees (restored when the mission ends).
- New zombies: slow, hard-hitting Brutes guard the valves; fast Runners surge in when half the time is gone. Walkers as before.
- Safe Zone ring at the Outbreak Post: zombies stop chasing inside it and you heal faster.
- Crashed, burning wrecks with smoke and embers, barricades, blood stains and rubble.
- Applies to Paris, Tokyo, New York and Bangkok (screenshot-checked in Paris only).

Validation (Mac): parse check, screenshots, import, smoke, save restore. Not yet checked on the Fold5 (smoke and embers cost on phone unknown).
