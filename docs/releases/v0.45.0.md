# Harbor City 0.45.0: brand-new hover cars

- **Five new hover cars made in Blender**, replacing the old shapes everywhere (your car, the showroom, traffic and races):
  - **Lumen**: a retro-future hatchback with big rounded thruster covers, cyan ring lamps and a glowing skirt line.
  - **Solar Cab**: a wedge-nosed city cab with a tall glass cabin and two big rear thruster pods with vents and glowing exhausts.
  - **Interceptor**: a tough angular cruiser with a two-tone nose, chrome guard rails, side vents and a red/blue roof light.
  - **Vela**: a long, sharp wedge racer with a full-width light bar, side intakes and a glowing tail strip.
  - **Arc**: the hypercar, with one great roof arch sweeping over a glass bubble and blades front and back.
- **Underglow**: every hover car throws a soft cyan glow on the road underneath, best at night.
- Your paint colours and second colour still apply to every car.
