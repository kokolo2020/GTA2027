# Chit Chat City 0.59.2 - WORLD PORTAL button, portal moved to the Ridge

- New on-screen WORLD PORTAL button (bottom right, above CALL CAR). One tap takes you to Paris. In Paris the same button reads PORTAL HOME and brings you back. It hides when driving, riding, in menus and on the clean screen.
- The portal gate moved out of the water to the Ridge lawn, north of the Ridge Pass road (open to everyone). It keeps its light beam, and Menu > GO > Paris portal still takes you to it.
- Everything else from 0.59.0 is unchanged.

Validation (Mac): Paris test run before release. Not yet checked on the Fold5.
