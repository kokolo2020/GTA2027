# Harbor City 0.33.4: players look like themselves

- **Your look, everywhere**: other online players now appear as the same character you play, wearing the shirt, pants and hat they picked at the Style Shop. Everyone sees the same person on every phone, tablet and browser. Players on an older version keep a random look until they update.
- **No more giant name tags**: when another player stands right at your camera or just behind it, their name tag is hidden instead of filling the screen. It comes back when they step in front of you.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed, including checks that your look is sent and that other players' shirts, pants and hats show and update. Not yet tried with two real devices.
