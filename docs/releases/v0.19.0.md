# Harbor City 0.19.0 — Civic Center, nicknames, dome zoom

- **Civic Center** (new, open from the start): the starting area is now more than twice as big. Follow the waterfront street west from the old city. Police Station, Harbor Hospital (helipad, ambulance bay), Harbor High School (clock tower, basketball court), Grand Hotel, Seaside Hotel, Civic Mall, Civic Park (fountain, playground), Harbor Bank, Fire Station (with fire truck) and City Library. Streets with traffic lights, its own traffic and more people. 10 discovery sites (+$1,500 for all), a checkpoint, and it shows on the map.
- **Nicknames**: Menu > Nickname. 3-14 characters, rude names are blocked; shown on your name tag, in chat and in invites. You are asked once when you first go Online.
- **Harbor Dome zoom**: the camera eases in as you walk up to a table, the bar, the stage or the lounge, and stays close while you sit.
- **Invites from tables**: "Looking for players" now works only while seated at a PLAYER VS PLAYER table; ACCEPT seats you straight into the chair opposite the inviter.
- Fixes: Fountain Plaza (Marina) is reachable (every discovery site is now checked for reachability); buttons reposition correctly after folding or unfolding the phone mid-game.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (Civic sites reachable, lanes drivable, traffic and signals, nickname rules and saving, invite seating, dome zoom). Not yet tested on the Fold5 or with two real phones for invites.
