# Harbor City 0.29.0: a clean screen, the North Highway rebuilt big, real street lights

- **Clean screen**: only the QR code (top left) and a small minimap (top right) stay on screen. Tap the minimap to open the Menu, which now holds every other button: Map, Quests, Travel, Call car, Chat, View, Radio, Online and Run. The joystick stays, and the big action button only shows when there is something to do right there (Enter car, Board bus, Sky train, Pick up and so on). Menu > Screen switches back to the full screen if you want it.
- **Running by default** on foot (Menu > Run to walk).
- **North Highway rebuilt big**: the highway is now 4 lanes, with two 4-lane avenues and cross streets that line up with Downtown's, so the two districts join as one grid.
  - **Industrial Quarter:** bigger warehouses.
  - **Maple Suburbs:** bigger houses, plus shops and apartments along the highway. Maple Court is now a small park, and the Skate Plaza has its own block.
  - **Midtown:** new towers next to Downtown.
  - **Harbor Airfield:** its own runway with hangars, planes and a control tower. The Runway Dash time trial now runs on it.
  - **Traffic and people:** more traffic, working traffic lights and more people on the pavements.
- **Real street lights everywhere**: tall traffic lights on slim poles with long arms that hang the lights over the lanes, and 9 m street lamps that reach out over the road and light it at night.
- **Sky train** pillars now also stand in the middle of the 4-lane roads.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed:
- the clean screen and the minimap Menu
- running by default
- every North Highway lane is clear to drive
- all earlier checks

Desktop frame rate is about 73 fps, or 85 fps with the browser settings.

Not yet tried on the Fold5 or in a real browser. The Old City, Civic Center, Westside, Marina and Sunset Beach are still the old size.
