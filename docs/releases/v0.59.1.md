# Chit Chat City 0.59.1 - Easier to find the Paris portal

- A tall light beam stands over the Paris portal (south of the Harbor Dome), so you can spot it from across the city.
- New Menu > GO > Paris portal button takes you straight to the portal. Then tap ENTER PORTAL: PARIS.
- Everything else from 0.59.0 (real Paris streets around the Eiffel Tower, Cafe de la Tour, 4 daily quests, return portal) is unchanged.
