HCHARBOR CITYRUN & TRAVEL / 0.23.0

KOKO'S ANDROID PROTOTYPE

Run the streets.
Jump across town.

New in 0.23: tap RUN to sprint on foot, and tap Travel to jump straight to any district you have opened, with your car. Delivery pick-ups and drop-offs now sit on the pavement beside the road, trees and street lamps stand taller, the walking arrow sits at your feet, and hats fit the new heads. From 0.22: on foot the camera drops to street level just behind your shoulder, so walking feels like a real city. Drag to look around. Left your car somewhere? Tap CALL CAR and it pulls up right beside you. Everyone in the city also got a makeover, with real faces, bending knees and elbows, and four hairstyles. From 0.21: Harbor Raceway is rebuilt as a real 1.4 km circuit with a chicane, hairpin, kerbs, gravel traps, grandstands and a pit lane with 15 garages. Park your own car in the big lot inside the gate, take the race car at the Pit Desk, and race a Grand Prix against robots driving the exact same car, or online racers. Boosters and spike strips still work. From 0.20: Harbor Raceway opened east of the North Highway. New in 0.19: the Civic Center doubles the starting area with a police station, hospital, school, hotels, bank, fire station, library, mall and park. Pick your own nickname, and the camera zooms in at Harbor Dome tables. New in 0.18.1: tap Chat, then Looking for players! to invite everyone online, who can accept and teleport straight to the dome. New in 0.18: Harbor Dome, a giant glass social hub with tic-tac-toe and rock-paper-scissors tables, a robot comedy show, quick-chat and emotes, plus quest arrows, checkpoints and a first-person Eye view. From 0.17: a minimap and full world map, an in-car view where you drive from the driver's seat, and a big celebration every time you level up. From 0.16: the Skyline District, a huge downtown of skyscrapers, a stadium, a train station, a mall and Central Park, plus real traffic that stops at working traffic lights and a much busier old city. As the thief, police roadblocks and a helicopter still hunt you. The Airport Line bus ($15 fare) now runs from Marina Heights to the Airfield. Everyone on the streets is a real walking character. Reach Level 5 to cross the new south bridge into Marina Heights: a car showroom with three new supercars, a style shop for your driver's shirt, pants and hat, a paint shop for your cars, five discovery sites, a new time trial and two missions. Player levels come from your quests, trials and discoveries. Chase mode: run from three police cars as the thief, or catch a fleeing thief as the police. Also try the experimental Online button to see other connected drivers in the city. Your money, cars and exploration progress stay local, and the complete North Update remains available in single player.

ONLINE PREVIEW

Drive together.
Keep your progress.

Chase mode (Menu > Chase): be the thief and survive 90 seconds, or be the police and arrest the thief. Wins pay $600; being busted never costs money. Online starts off. Tap Online to connect to the configured lobby; other players appear as non-colliding cars or on-foot markers. Live two-device online behavior still needs testing. Discover three Bay sites to open the new North Gate at the top of the city. Beyond it: a long highway, the Industrial Quarter, Maple Suburbs, the Harbor Airfield and the Summit Pass lookout, with eight new discovery sites, two new time trials (North Highway, Runway Dash) and a new mission. City buildings, houses, warehouses, hangars, planes, trees and lamps are all new Blender models. Your money, cars, tuning and progress are kept. This update passed automated desktop checks; testing on your Fold5 remains.

  1. Update the APK

    Open this page in Chrome on your phone. Download, then open Harbor-City-Android-debug.apk. Install it as an update over Harbor City; do not uninstall first if you want to keep your money. If Android asks, allow Chrome to install this app.

  2. Turn your phone sideways

    Use the left joystick to move. Follow the golden ring to your next goal. Own the Ridge Rover to open the east gate. Discover all six Ridge sites to open the west Bay Gate; three Bay sites open the north. Trials start from Menu.

  3. Start your collection

    Ridge sites are found just by arriving. Blue city tokens still need Collect Token on foot. Menu switches between exploring and delivery jobs.
