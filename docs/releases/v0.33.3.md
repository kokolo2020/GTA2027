# Harbor City 0.33.3: quests start by themselves

- **Automatic quests**: you no longer need to accept a quest. The nearest daily quest starts by itself, with the quest bar at the top and an arrow showing the way.
- **One after another**: when you finish a quest you get paid and the next nearest quest starts straight away.
- **Skip**: tap ✕ on the quest bar to skip a quest. It won't come back for 5 minutes, and the next one starts instead.
- The QUESTS button still lists every quest if you'd rather pick one yourself. Quests pause during races, trials, chases and quizzes.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.
