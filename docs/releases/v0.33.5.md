# Harbor City 0.33.5: track your friends, collect by walking

- **Track a player**: in PLAYERS, tap TRACK next to anyone online. Your arrow points at them and follows them as they move, showing their name and distance. Tap STOP to end it; it also stops if they go offline.
- **Quests complete when you arrive**: reach a daily quest spot and it pays out straight away, and the next quest starts. No PICK UP button needed.
- **Floating gems collect themselves**: walk into one of the eight floating blue city gems (Ferry Steps, Clocktower and the rest) in any mode to collect it.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.
