# Harbor City 0.37.0: the Biosphere

- **The Biosphere** opens on the empty land north-west of the Skyline District: a lit walk and gate from the boulevard, a glass hub with a hologram globe, and four giant geodesic domes joined to the hub and to each other by glass tunnels. Ideas from real biospheres: the Eden Project and Montreal's Biosphere (geodesic domes), the Biodome de Montreal (sub-polar zone and maple forest), Gardens by the Bay's Cloud Forest (waterfall mountain) and Biosphere 2 (savanna and coral ocean).
  - **Winter Dome**: frozen lake, iceberg and penguin slide, snowy pines, an igloo, northern lights and falling snow. Emperor Penguins, Polar Bears, Arctic Foxes, Snowy Owls.
  - **Spring Dome**: misty waterfall mountain with ferns and orchids, lily pond, cherry blossoms, tulip beds, drifting petals. Spotted Deer, Rabbits, Mallard Ducks, Butterflies.
  - **Summer Dome**: coral lagoon with a palm island, savanna with acacias and termite mounds, a glowing sun. Giraffes, Zebras, Flamingos, Macaw Parrots.
  - **Autumn Dome**: maple forest, beaver lodge and dam, mushrooms, pumpkins, falling leaves. Moose, Raccoons, Beavers, Canada Geese flying in a V.
- Visitors walk a ring path in each dome; animals wander, swim and fly in fenced habitats, each with a fact sign.
- **Animal spotting**: walk near an animal you can see to spot it. Each complete dome pays $300 and 60 XP; all 16 pay $1,000 and 200 XP more. New achievements: Animal Spotter and Biosphere Explorer. On the minimap and city map.
- **More detailed people** (same style): eyes with coloured irises and highlights, eyelids, arched brows, lips, nose and chin, fuller hair, hands with fingers, sneakers with laces, cuffs, shirt hem and belt buckle.
- **Airport**: parked jets are a little smaller so neighbouring wing tips no longer touch.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.
