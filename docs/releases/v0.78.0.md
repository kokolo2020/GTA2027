# Bonkers City: Boss Night! 0.78.0 - Roles, an alien boss and new boss arenas

- **Choose your role at every portal** (and when you tap JOIN): Healer, Tank, DPS, Mage, Warlock or Hunter.
  - Healer: the only role that can heal in a boss fight (HEAL WAVE, plus a slow heal on yourself).
  - Tank: double health and the shield slam.
  - DPS: double damage.
  - Mage: frost slows the boss, and a pet fights for you.
  - Warlock: poison keeps burning the boss, and a pet fights for you.
  - Hunter: a much stronger pet.
- **No more free healing in boss fights.** Safe zones and slow regeneration are off while a boss is awake.
- **Pets really fight now** (Mage, Warlock, Hunter): big shots at minions and the boss, with a damage number. If you own no pet you get a free one in the fight.
- **THE WORLD EATER is a giant alien** with black eyes, a glowing heart and four clawed arms.
- **New boss arenas:** tall themed trees for each city (ember, neon sakura, crystal, mangrove) and rocks.

Validation (Mac): parse checks, full Paris tests, self-test (V72_CHECK OK: roles, healing rules, pets), Ultimate fight test, and real renders of the role screen, the alien and all four arenas. Not checked on the Fold5.
