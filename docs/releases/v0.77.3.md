# Bonkers City: Boss Night! 0.77.3 - Your pet fights in boss fights

- If you have a pet (Robo Pup, Hover Cat, Mini Dragon or Star Fox), it now dashes at the boss's minions and zaps them, which also charges your BLAST a little.
- When no minions are left, your pet nips at the boss.

Validation (Mac): parse checks and self-test (V72_CHECK OK, including the pet fight). Not checked on the Fold5, and other players' pets do not fight yet.
