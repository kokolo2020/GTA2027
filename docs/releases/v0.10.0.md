# Harbor City 0.10.0 — Chase Update

New Chase mode (Menu > Chase), offline:
- THIEF: escape three AI police cars for 90 seconds. Win $600. If a cop stays within 5 m for 2.5 s you are busted; no money is ever lost.
- POLICE: catch a fleeing thief car by staying within 5 m for 2.5 s. Win $600.
- Flashing lights, name tags, a wanted/arrest meter and a direction arrow on screen.

Includes the 0.9.0 optional online mode. Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate.
