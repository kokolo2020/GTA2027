# Harbor City 0.17.0 — Minimap, in-car view, level-up celebration

- **Minimap** (top right): the whole world seen from above, turning with your view. Shows you, the golden ring (pinned to the edge when far away), undiscovered sites as coloured dots and police during chases.
- **Full map**: tap the minimap for a north-up map of the whole world with district names. Close map to carry on.
- **Camera views**: the new View button cycles Far (as before), Close, and **In-car**: drive from the driver's seat with a dashboard, steering wheel and your car's bonnet. In-car, up is gas, down brakes then reverses, left/right steer. Far buildings fade into a light haze there.
- **Level-up celebration**: gold flash, a big LEVEL UP! banner with spinning rays, a golden light pillar, a ripple ring, confetti and a rising jingle.
- Includes 0.16's Skyline District, traffic with traffic lights and busier streets.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; money, cars and progress are kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (map opens and closes, in-car camera and gas/steer/brake/reverse, views switch back, level-up banner/effect/jingle). HUD checked at the folded (wide) and unfolded screen shapes in renders. Not yet tested on the Fold5: in-car driving feel on touch, jingle volume, performance.
