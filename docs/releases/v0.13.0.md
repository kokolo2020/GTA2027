# Harbor City 0.13.0 — Marina Heights

Reach Level 5 to open the south bridge into Marina Heights, a new district:
- **Harbor Motors Showroom**: three new premium cars, Marina GT ($7,500), Skyline Racer ($12,000) and Phantom Black ($18,000), the fastest cars in the game.
- **Style Shop** (on foot): customize your driver's shirt, pants and hat, from a free cap to a $800 crown.
- **Paint Shop** (drive in): repaint any car you own in 10 colors for $150, or go back to factory colors.
- Five discovery sites (Bridge Midpoint, Fountain Plaza, Yacht Club, Sunset Boardwalk, Marina Lighthouse) with a $3,000 bonus for all five.
- New time trial: Marina Boulevard (par 9 s, $900).
- Two new missions: Marina Arrival ($500) and Window Shopper ($800).

Includes the 0.11 online police/thief roles and the 0.12 player levels. Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; your money, cars, tuning and progress are kept.

Debug-signed test build. Desktop smoke test passed (including Marina Heights shops, levels and online roles). Not yet tested on the Fold5: shop panels on the touch screen, new car handling at top speed, Marina performance.
