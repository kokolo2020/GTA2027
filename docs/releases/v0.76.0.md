# Bonkers City: Boss Night! 0.76.0 - Four bosses and a victory party

- Every city boss is different now:
  - Paris, THE IRON GHOUL: dark red, black horns. Signature attack METEOR RAIN: fireballs fall around you.
  - Tokyo, THE NEON ONI: purple and neon, long swept horns, smaller and much faster, less health. Signature NEON RAIL: a straight row of blasts from the boss through you; step sideways.
  - New York, THE TIMES TITAN: black and gold with a crown, the biggest and slowest, the most health. Signature TITAN STOMP: everything close to the boss explodes; get away from it.
  - Bangkok, THE RIVER GIANT: teal with a crest of fins and tusks, sends bigger waves of minions. Signature RIVER CROSS: a plus-shaped pattern centred on you; the corners are safe.
  Each has its own flame colour, lair glow, minion colour and music pitch. All still use the ground slam too.
- Victory: every player in the fight gets $100,000 and goes straight up to the next level, with a party: VICTORY on screen, fireworks over the lair for 10 seconds, a fountain of gold coins, golden rings, a pillar of light, a fanfare, and your character cheers. Everyone is healed.
  - The $100,000 and the level are for the first win in each city each day (so up to four a day). More wins in the same city that day pay $10,000.
- Losing: when time runs out, TIME'S UP shows and the whole team is sent home to the Portal Summit.
- The call to action now reads JOIN THE FIGHT NOW! on the banner, in the message and on the Harbor Live news screen.

Validation (Mac): smoke test, portal-city test, self-test (four bosses with their own health and attack, 100,000 + level on the first win, 10,000 on the second, sent home on a timeout), real renders of all four bosses and the victory. Not checked on the Fold5 or with three real players.
