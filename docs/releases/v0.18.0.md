# Harbor City 0.18.0 — Harbor Dome, quest arrows, checkpoints, Eye view

- **Harbor Dome** (new social hub): a giant glass dome on the water east of the old city. Take the causeway east from the waterfront street; open to everyone from the start.
  - Tic-tac-toe ($10, win $20) and rock-paper-scissors ($5, win $10) tables: play the robot regulars, or another online player at the PLAYER VS PLAYER tables.
  - Comedy stage: a robot comedian tells 5 original jokes for $5, spoken with your phone's voice, with subtitles.
  - Lounge, café, a colour-cycling dance floor, and robot regulars who chat and wave.
  - Chat button: quick phrases and emotes (wave, clap, dance, cheer), shown to other online players too. Walk up to any seat and tap SIT.
- **Quest arrows**: a gold arrow over you points at your current goal, a big arrow marks the goal, and a screen arrow with the distance shows in In-car and Eye views.
- **Checkpoints**: 10 glowing pads around the map. Step on one to save; you start there next time and "stuck" rescues return you there.
- **Eye view** when walking (View button: Far, Close, Eye) with drag-to-look, and a walk-in **Harbor Stadium** with a crowd and scoreboard.
- **In-car fix**: the car now drives where it points (no more sliding), and small sideways thumb movement no longer steers.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; money, cars and progress are kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (dome tables, payouts, simulated online match, comedy show, chat/emotes, arrows, checkpoints, stadium, eye and in-car driving). Not yet tested: two real phones online together, the phone's speaking voice, Fold5 feel and performance.
