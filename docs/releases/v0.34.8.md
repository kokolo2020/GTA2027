# Harbor City 0.34.8: car fixes

- **Fix: checkpoint loop.** After your parked car went home, you could be sent back to your checkpoint over and over until you called the car. The hidden car was falling through the ground and setting off the "fell out of the world" rescue. It now stays put, and a rescue also brings the car back.
- **Travel on foot leaves your car at home.** Travelling no longer drops your car beside you (for example on the grass in Central Park). Tap CALL CAR when you want it. If you travel while driving, your car still comes with you.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed, including a check that a car that went home stays put and doesn't move you.
