# Harbor City 0.34: hover cars, hoverboard, Neon Market and leaderboard

- **Hover cars**: every car (yours, traffic and race cars) floats on four glowing jets with a neon strip along the sides. Driving, races and chases work the same.
- **Hoverboard**: on foot, tap BOARD (next to the big button) to ride a glowing hoverboard, faster than running. Tap again to step off.
- **Neon Market** (old city, east lawn across from the food court):
  - Neon Outfitters: Neon Visor, Glow Headphones, Golden Halo, Jetpack, Angel Wings and Royal Crown, worn with your hat.
  - Pet Palace: Robo Pup, Hover Cat, Mini Dragon and Star Fox. Pets unlock at level 20 and follow you everywhere.
  - Everyone online sees your gear and pet.
- **Your money on screen**: your cash shows under the minimap. Tap it for the leaderboard.
- **Leaderboard** (also in Menu): live top 30 for Level, Money, Achievements and Kindness, shared by every player with a nickname. Scores update every minute or two.
- **Cleaner name tags**: you no longer see your own tag (your ring marks you), and other players show just their name and level. Hearts, diamonds and crews are in PLAYERS.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed, including the market, the pet level lock, other players' gear and pets, hoverboard speed, the money display and the leaderboard list. The leaderboard was tested live against the Supabase table (post and read back). Not yet tried on a phone.
