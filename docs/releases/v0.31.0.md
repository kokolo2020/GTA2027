# Harbor City 0.31.0: Neo Harbor

Futuristic city
- **Neon light strips**: glowing cyan LED bands wrap every building at the roof and every few floors on towers, fitted to each building's shape. They're soft by day and bright at night, with a glow effect.
- **Holograms**: spinning hologram rings with floating names over Times Plaza (NEO HARBOR), Union Station, the Grand Mall, Midtown and the Old City. Every billboard is now a glowing hologram screen.
- **Flying traffic**: hover cars with glowing thrusters fly in sky lanes over Downtown, the North Highway and the coast, and delivery drones carry parcels lower down. They're scenery only.
- **LED streets**: cool white LED street lamps, and lane lines and crossings that glow faintly at night.

Quests and players (from 0.30.1)
- **Quests pop up near you**: walk or drive within 40 m of a daily quest and a card offers it, with ACCEPT or NO THANKS. Once you accept, a bar at the top shows the quest and distance (tap it to stop), and the arrow shows the way.
- **Real players stand out**: a pulsing glowing ring (gold for you, cyan for others online), your name and level over you on the clean screen, and an "N ONLINE" count under the minimap.
- **Nothing to collect stands in the road**: 17 gems, discovery sites and quest items moved onto pavements. The blue slabs at the Old City crossings are gone, and Bridge Midpoint is now Bridge Gate at the bridge entrance.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed, including new checks for quest offers and for nothing on the roads. Desktop frame rate is about 56 fps, or 77 fps with the browser settings (the browser skips the glow). Not yet tried on the Fold5, in a real browser, or with phones online together.
