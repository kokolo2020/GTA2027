ONLINE PREVIEW

Drive together.
Keep your progress.

Chase mode (Menu > Chase): be the thief and survive 90 seconds, or be the police and arrest the thief. Wins pay $600; being busted never costs money. Online starts off. Tap Online to connect to the configured lobby; other players appear as non-colliding cars or on-foot markers. Live two-device online behavior still needs testing. Discover three Bay sites to open the new North Gate at the top of the city. Beyond it: a long highway, the Industrial Quarter, Maple Suburbs, the Harbor Airfield and the Summit Pass lookout, with eight new discovery sites, two new time trials (North Highway, Runway Dash) and a new mission. City buildings, houses, warehouses, hangars, planes, trees and lamps are all new Blender models. Your money, cars, tuning and progress are kept. This update passed automated desktop checks; testing on your Fold5 remains.

  1. Update the APK

    Open this page in Chrome on your phone. Download, then open Harbor-City-Android-debug.apk. Install it as an update over Chit Chat City; do not uninstall first if you want to keep your money. If Android asks, allow Chrome to install this app.

  2. Turn your phone sideways

    Use the left joystick to move. Follow the golden ring to your next goal. Own the Ridge Rover to open the east gate. Discover all six Ridge sites to open the west Bay Gate; three Bay sites open the north. Trials start from Menu.

  3. Start your collection

    Ridge sites are found just by arriving. Blue city tokens still need Collect Token on foot. Menu switches between exploring and delivery jobs.
