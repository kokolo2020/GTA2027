# Harbor City 0.56.2 - Christmas Harbor Dome

- The Harbor Dome is now Christmas: snow falls inside the whole dome, a giant lit tree with presents, a ring of lit trees, three rings of string lights, snowmen by the comedy stage and a MERRY CHRISTMAS sign.
- New players start in the middle of the Harbor Dome. Chapter 1 now begins with two guided steps (Dome gate, Ridge Pass) and the arrow leads them to the city. Existing saves skip the two new steps.
- The Sun Dome from 0.56.1 is removed; the Food Court spawn check is restored. The hourly challenge has no terminal for now.

Validation (Mac): focused and full smoke passed; dome screenshots checked. Phone FPS with the snow is unverified.
