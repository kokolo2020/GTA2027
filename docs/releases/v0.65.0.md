# Chit Chat City 0.65.0 - Real zombies, health bar, big timer

- Outbreak missions: zombies are now real person models (green skin, torn clothes, red eyes) that shamble toward you when you get close. Survivors are real people waving for help.
- Health bar: bites drain health (about 22/s); health slowly recovers when nothing touches you and +25 per survivor rescued. If you die you wake up at that world's portal and the mission ends.
- A huge countdown at the top of the screen starts the moment the rescue starts; it turns yellow, then red, and pulses.
- Zombies do not collide with walls yet.

Validation (Mac): parse check, all-world screenshot test including a scripted death in each world, import, smoke, save restore. Touch controls not yet checked on the Fold5.
