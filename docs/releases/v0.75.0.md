# Bonkers City: Boss Night! 0.75.0 - Roles

- Choose a role at the Portal Summit: three role stones stand on the rock. Walk up to one and tap the button. You can change any time; the choice is saved.
  - MAGE (damage): the CURE BLAST as before, the biggest hit on the boss.
  - TANK (guardian): double health. Special: SHIELD SLAM, 7 seconds of shield that cuts damage by 80%, cures everything around you and hits the boss.
  - HEALER (medic): special: HEAL WAVE, heals you to full and gives 60 health to every player within 22 m, and cures the infected close by.
- The special button shows your role's move (BLAST, SHIELD or HEAL) and charges the same way: by shooting.
- Everyone starts as a MAGE, so nothing changes until you pick.

Validation (Mac): smoke test, portal-city test, self-test (role stones, tank health and shield, healer wave in and out of range), real render of the stones. Not checked on the Fold5; the heal reaching another real player has not been tried with two devices.
