# Harbor City 0.54.2: World Biosphere doors and gates

Every continent dome now has a clear door, about 15 m wide, centred on its tunnel, plus its own gate arch with the continent name. Before, the door was cut from fixed wall pieces and could sit off-centre, so the wall and the tunnel's side rails could block the way in. There is also a new entrance arch where the walk from the city meets the hub.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Validation: desktop import, World Biosphere check and smoke test. Not yet checked on a phone.
