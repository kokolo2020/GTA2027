# Harbor City 0.50.1: better homes, no more getting stuck

- **Fix**: you could get stuck inside your home after decorating, because the new piece appeared right where you stood. Each glowing decorating spot is now in front of its furniture, never on top of it.
- **Homes have proper rooms**: a living room with the kitchen, fridge and dining table; a bedroom with a bed; and a bathroom with a toilet, sink, mirror and glass shower. Bigger homes get the same rooms, larger, with more spots to decorate. Everything you already bought stays (each piece moves to a spot of the same kind).
- **Driver's seat**: getting into your car now puts the camera in the driver's seat (change it any time in Menu > SETTINGS > View).

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (every decorating spot is clear of furniture, old layouts keep their pieces, entering the car switches to the driver's view). Not yet checked on a phone.
