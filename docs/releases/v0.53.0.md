# Harbor City 0.53.0: new character trial

Try a new playable character with adult proportions, a softer face, a futuristic tech outfit,
and a skinned skeleton for walking, running, sitting and gestures. Running uses a retargeted
existing animation with revised arms, curled hands, shoulder rotation and a steadier gaze.
Other people in the city and online peers retain their current appearance.

**Easy revert:** Menu > Settings > Character switches between New and Classic immediately.
Your choice is remembered after restarting. Classic restores your saved wardrobe appearance.
The new character currently wears one fixed tech outfit; wardrobe choices and purchases
remain saved, and the wardrobe screen explains the trial's clothing limitation.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Money, cars, upgrades, homes, quests,
discoveries and other saved progress are retained. Existing package and signing identity.
Debug-signed Android test build, plus the updated browser version.

Validation: Godot import, focused avatar checks, a full desktop smoke test, and rendered
in-game screenshots. New/Classic switching and classic startup fallback checked on desktop.
Not yet checked on a phone; animation quality and mobile performance remain trial feedback.
