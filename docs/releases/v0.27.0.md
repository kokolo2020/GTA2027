# Harbor City 0.27.0: One city, sky train, airport, day and night

- **No more walls**: the rock walls around every district are gone. The empty gaps between districts are now grass and trees, and new roads join everything up: Raceway Road beside the Raceway, the Coast Road from the Raceway to Sunset Beach, Westside Boulevard between Westside and Downtown, the North Highway streets running east, Westside to Bay Road, and the Civic Center avenues north to the Bay. The sea is now the edge of the world: drive or walk in and you're towed back to the road.
- **Harbor Loop sky train**: a 2.7 km elevated line 15 m up, with 5 stations: Harbor Waterfront, Sunset Beach, Harbor Raceway, Union Station and Westside Market. Walk into the blue ring under a station and tap SKY TRAIN to go up to the platform, and the next train picks you up. Two trains run the loop, so you wait 2 minutes at most, and every player sees them in the same place. Tap GET OFF NEXT to hop off at the next stop. View: Eye gives you a window seat.
- **City Tour**: visit all 5 stations by train in one day for $750 + 150 XP (once a day).
- **Harbor International Airport**, north of the city on Airport Road (new fast-travel point, Harbor Airport):
  - a 740 m runway with edge and approach lights, and a taxiway
  - a glass terminal with 5 gates and jet bridges, plus a car park
  - a control tower, 3 hangars, a cargo yard and a fuel farm
  - airliners at every gate, plus one that lands, taxis and takes off again about every 3 minutes
  - baggage trains, a fuel truck, an airport bus and a follow-me car on the move
  - 23 ground crew in hi-vis
- **Day and night**: a game day lasts 24 minutes, with sunrise, daytime, sunset and night (stars and a moon). At night street lamps light the ground, windows and car lights glow, the sky train is lit and your car has headlights. A clock sits next to the minimap. Menu > Time lets you keep it always day, sunset or night.
- The world map is redrawn with the new roads, the train line and the airport.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.
