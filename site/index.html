Games, achievements and glory.
Play the dome, earn your colours.

New in 0.34.7: the old city is now futuristic glass towers (round, stepped, twisting and sky-garden towers), the road runs straight onto the Marina bridge so you can walk across, bus stops are glass shelters with live countdown screens, and City Loop buses drive all over town. From 0.34.5: Harbor City jumps 100 years ahead. Roads are smooth dark panels with glowing seams that become a neon grid at night, traffic lights are glowing strips set into the road instead of tall poles, buildings are sleek pearl panels with glowing floor bands and solar-glass roofs, street lamps are slim light blades, and the sky train runs on slim white columns with glass lift tubes. A parked car now goes home after 2 minutes so it never blocks the street (tap CALL CAR to get it back). From 0.34.3: streamer mode for livestreams (Menu > Streamer mode), with a LIVE badge, a big join code so viewers can scan and play with you, today's leaders across the top and a clean screen. From 0.34.2: Harbor Live opens by West Arcade, a neon plaza of giant screens with the live leaderboard, city news and announcements under a spinning hologram trophy. You can also wear all your Neon Outfitters gear at once. From 0.34.1: the Pet Palace, Neon Outfitters and leaderboard screens close properly. From 0.34: cars are hover cars that float on glowing jets, and on foot you can ride a hoverboard (tap BOARD). Shop the new Neon Market for visors, halos, jetpacks, wings and crowns, and from level 20 robotic pets that follow you everywhere. Your money now shows under the minimap, and tapping it opens a live leaderboard for level, money, achievements and kindness. Name tags are cleaner too: yours is hidden, and other players show just their name and level. From 0.33.6: the Harbor Food Court opens in the old city. Hover stands for fresh orange juice, hot dogs and smoothies float on neon rings, with spinning hologram treats, hologram menus, robot vendors, neon tables and busy crowds. From 0.33.5: tap PLAYERS and TRACK anyone online, and the arrow follows them around the city. Daily quests complete as soon as you reach the spot, and floating gems collect when you walk into them, with no button to press. From 0.33.4: other players look the way they dress, with their own shirt, pants and hat, so everyone sees the same person on every screen, and name tags no longer fill the screen when a player stands right by your camera. From 0.33.3: quests start by themselves. The nearest quest becomes yours with an arrow showing the way, the next one starts as soon as you finish, and tapping ✕ on the quest bar skips one. From 0.33.2: symbols and arrows show properly on iPad and in every browser, level 20 rainbow rings look the same on every device, and other players' names sit right above them. From 0.33.1: the minimap shows how many players are online right now, even before you join, and one tap puts you online with them. From 0.33: Harbor Dome is a game centre. Step into the Party Quiz ring for a quiz everyone plays together every few minutes, or hit the arcade for Connect Four, Reaction Duel and Memory Match against robots or friends. There are 91 achievements in Bronze, Silver, Gold and Legend with cash and XP rewards, and Gold and Legend unlocks are announced to everyone online. Your ring colour now shows your level, and your name and glow show your best achievement. Traffic lights are sleek LED light bars with an amber phase, and players far out at the airport or Westside now show up online. From 0.32: Harbor City is social. While online, tap PLAYERS to add friends, high-five, give gifts, invite players to where you are, or jump to your friends. Start a crew with its own name and colour, invite players to it, earn more from quests together, and see the crews online on the board. Kindness from gifts and high-fives shows on your name tag. Dance with everyone on the new Times Plaza dance floor, take a group photo from the Menu, and earn XP for hanging out with friends. From 0.31: Harbor City goes futuristic. Glowing neon strips wrap every building, holograms float over the plazas, billboards are hologram screens, hover cars and delivery drones fly in sky lanes above the streets, and the streets glow with LED lamps and lit lane lines at night. Quests now pop up when you get near one, so you can accept it or say no thanks, and an arrow shows the way. Real players stand on a glowing ring with their name over them (gold for you, cyan for others online), nothing to collect stands in the road any more, and the minimap shows how many players are online. From 0.30: Harbor City looks real. Roads, pavements, walls and roofs use photo textures (real asphalt, concrete, brick, stucco and clay tiles), glass towers have window frames and light up window by window at night, trees have round leafy crowns, the cars are rebuilt with real shapes and glossy paint, and the bus and Harbor Loop sky train are smooth and rounded. From 0.29.1: a QUESTS button under the QR code, so your daily quests are one tap away on the clean screen, and the big button shows CALL CAR again when your car is far away. From 0.29: a clean screen with just the QR code and a small minimap (tap the minimap for the Menu, where every other button now lives), and you run on foot by default. The North Highway is rebuilt at big-city scale with a 4-lane highway, 4-lane avenues and cross streets that line up with Downtown, Midtown towers, bigger Maple Suburbs houses and a Harbor Airfield with its own runway. Every street in the city has tall new traffic lights that hang over the lanes and 9 m street lamps that reach out over the road. From 0.28: the Skyline District is rebuilt at big-city scale. Every avenue and street is a 4-lane road with a double yellow centre line, lane markings, wide pavements and big crossings, the district is about 1.6 times wider, and the towers are about 1.7 times bigger (Harbor Tower now stands about 165 m tall). Central Park, Harbor Stadium, Union Station and the Grand Mall are all bigger too, and the airport road and Westside Boulevard join the downtown grid. More districts get the big-city treatment next. From 0.27.1: traffic no longer gets stuck behind people standing in the road. From 0.27: the walls are gone and new roads join every district, so you can drive anywhere on land (drive into the sea and you get towed back). Ride the Harbor Loop, an elevated sky train with five stations: Harbor Waterfront, Sunset Beach, Harbor Raceway, Union Station and Westside Market. Visit all five by train in one day for a $750 City Tour bonus. Harbor International Airport opens north of the city with a 740 m runway, a glass terminal with five gates, a control tower, hangars, airliners that land and take off, baggage trains, fuel trucks, buses and ground crew. The city now has real day and night: sunrise, sunset, stars, glowing street lamps and lit windows (Menu > Time to keep it day or night). From 0.26.2: Sunset Beach and Westside now have proper houses, the same as Maple Suburbs. From 0.26: two new districts make the city about 45% bigger. Drive over the new bridge to Sunset Beach for a beach boulevard, the Sunset Hotel, a boardwalk, a pier and a lighthouse, or head west through the Civic Center to Westside, with its market square, windmill park and water tower. Twice as many people now walk the streets, and there are six new daily quests. From 0.25.1: FIND ME counts a find anywhere on the glowing floor and shows finders their place, and the browser version runs smoother. From 0.25: quick quests are now 20 daily quests. Each one pays once a day, and every stop has the real thing waiting on the pavement: a coffee on a café table, a lost cat, a pot of flowers, a birthday cake, a kite and more. Finish all 20 for a $500 bonus; they all come back at midnight. From 0.24.2: every treasure hunt tells you what to look for, from a giant rubber duck to a hot-air balloon, and FIND ME puts you on the pavement instead of the road. From 0.24.1: a treasure hunt every 5 minutes with clues and a golden light beam, FIND ME! hide-and-seek with prize money for the first three finders, a Quests list, and name tags over every player. From 0.24: open a free starter crate for your first car and cash, then jump straight into quick quests that pay on the spot. Level up all the way to 20, every town is open from the start, the city has a new sky and richer colours, and a Scan to play QR code lets friends join from their phone browser. From 0.23: tap RUN to sprint on foot, and tap Travel to jump straight to any district you have opened, with your car. Delivery pick-ups and drop-offs now sit on the pavement beside the road, trees and street lamps stand taller, the walking arrow sits at your feet, and hats fit the new heads. From 0.22: on foot the camera drops to street level just behind your shoulder, so walking feels like a real city. Drag to look around. Left your car somewhere? Tap CALL CAR and it pulls up right beside you. Everyone in the city also got a makeover, with real faces, bending knees and elbows, and four hairstyles. From 0.21: Harbor Raceway is rebuilt as a real 1.4 km circuit with a chicane, hairpin, kerbs, gravel traps, grandstands and a pit lane with 15 garages. Park your own car in the big lot inside the gate, take the race car at the Pit Desk, and race a Grand Prix against robots driving the exact same car, or online racers. Boosters and spike strips still work. From 0.20: Harbor Raceway opened east of the North Highway. New in 0.19: the Civic Center doubles the starting area with a police station, hospital, school, hotels, bank, fire station, library, mall and park. Pick your own nickname, and the camera zooms in at Harbor Dome tables. New in 0.18.1: tap Chat, then Looking for players! to invite everyone online, who can accept and teleport straight to the dome. New in 0.18: Harbor Dome, a giant glass social hub with tic-tac-toe and rock-paper-scissors tables, a robot comedy show, quick-chat and emotes, plus quest arrows, checkpoints and a first-person Eye view. From 0.17: a minimap and full world map, an in-car view where you drive from the driver's seat, and a big celebration every time you level up. From 0.16: the Skyline District, a huge downtown of skyscrapers, a stadium, a train station, a mall and Central Park, plus real traffic that stops at working traffic lights and a much busier old city. As the thief, police roadblocks and a helicopter still hunt you. The Airport Line bus ($15 fare) now runs from Marina Heights to the Airfield. Everyone on the streets is a real walking character.   1. Update the APK

    Open this page in Chrome on your phone. Download, then open Harbor-City-Android-debug.apk. Install it as an update over Harbor City; do not uninstall first if you want to keep your money. If Android asks, allow Chrome to install this app.

  2. Turn your phone sideways

    Use the left joystick to move. Follow the golden ring to your next goal. Own the Ridge Rover to open the east gate. Discover all six Ridge sites to open the west Bay Gate; three Bay sites open the north. Trials start from Menu.

  3. Start your collection

    Ridge sites are found just by arriving. Blue city tokens still need Collect Token on foot. Menu switches between exploring and delivery jobs.
