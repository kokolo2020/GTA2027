# Harbor City 0.54.0: World Biosphere

The Biosphere moves to open land far west of the city and grows into the World Biosphere: a central hub with seven continent domes in a star (about 77 m across each) and room for a big Ocean World dome later. Africa, Asia, South America and Antarctica are open now; the other three domes are shells for a later update.

- **Landscapes:** Africa (savanna, watering hole, kopje rocks, acacias), Asia (bamboo, misty mountains, rice terraces, a temple-style pavilion), South America (rainforest, waterfall, river, canopy bridges), Antarctica (ice cliffs, frozen sea, snow cave, aurora).
- **Animals:** new Blender models including lion, elephant, rhino, meerkat, panda, tiger, red panda, snow leopard, crane, jaguar, sloth, toucan, monkey, poison frog, seals, orca and albatross.
- **Come back every day:** Biosphere Passport (stamps, badges, World Explorer), Ranger ranks, dome quests, feeding shows, Photo Safari, daily keeper task with streak, rare sightings, baby animal events, friend co-op bonuses and new achievements.
- **Fast:** only the dome you are in is drawn in full detail.
- Opens at level 7. Fast travel, Directions, the map, signposts and the sky-train stop point to the new place. Animals you already spotted are kept.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Validation: desktop import, World Biosphere check, full smoke test and benchmark (about 181 fps in the new dome area on the Mac). Not yet checked on a phone.
