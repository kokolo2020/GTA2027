# Harbor City 0.26.0: Sunset Beach, Westside and busier streets

- **Two new districts, about 45% more land**:
  - **Sunset Beach**, a new island south-east of Harbor Dome. A bridge branches south off the Dome causeway (before the Dome's car barrier), so you can drive there. It has a shopping boulevard, beach houses, the Sunset Hotel, a sandy beach with umbrellas, towels and palms, a boardwalk, a pier with a kiosk and a lighthouse.
  - **Westside**, west of the Civic Center: drive straight through on the Civic Center's middle street. It has streets of houses with green yards, the Westside Market (six stalls and a fountain), a windmill park and a water tower.
  - Both are on the minimap, the world map and the Travel menu, and each has a traffic loop.
- **Twice as many people**: 153 pedestrians instead of 78, with crowds on the beach, boardwalk, pier and market, and more in the old city, Skyline District and Civic Center. People far from you update in cheaper steps, so the frame rate holds (desktop check: 72 fps, 84 fps with the browser setup).
- **6 new daily quests** (26 in all): ice cream at Sunset Pier, a surfboard on the beach, a postcard at the lighthouse, fresh bread at Westside Market, sunflowers at the windmill and a lost cat on Westside Hill.
