# Chit Chat City 0.68.0 - Quest tracker

- QUESTS panel in every city portal world: quests, clues, case, outbreak and the way home, with distances and done marks.
- Yellow arrow and a tall light beam point to your next goal; tap any row to pick a different target.
- During outbreaks the arrow leads you to survivors and valves in order.
