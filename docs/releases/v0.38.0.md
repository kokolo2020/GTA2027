# Harbor City 0.38.0: the Biosphere, redesigned

- **Hexagon domes**: every dome and the hub is now a six-sided glass gridshell with a triangle frame (ideas from the Eden Project biomes and Montreal's Biosphere). Each dome has its own shape and colours: a tall pointed ice dome for Winter, a rounded pink dome for Spring, a low gold dome for Summer and a copper-framed dome for Autumn.
- **Sections, not open space**: each dome is six sections round a fenced central habitat, with a visitor ring path. The three far sections are separate fenced enclosures, one animal each with its own landscape:
  - Winter: Penguin Ice Pool, Polar Coast, Arctic Tundra, Snowy Owl Woods
  - Spring: Rain Vortex Pool, Blossom Meadow, Bunny Burrows, Butterfly Garden
  - Summer: Coral Lagoon, Acacia Plains, Zebra Grassland, Macaw Aviary
  - Autumn: Beaver Pond, Maple Forest, Raccoon Hollow, Goose Marsh
- **Skywalks**: two ramps beside each dome's entrance climb to a walkway 4 m up that loops over the enclosures, with glass railings and a glass-floored lookout over the far enclosure (Glacier Skywalk, Cloud Walk, Safari Skywalk, Canopy Boardwalk).
- **Rain vortex**: in the Spring Dome, water pours from an opening in the roof into the duck pool, wrapped in mist (idea from Jewel Changi, Singapore).
- Birds fly only over their own enclosure; the geese now live in the Goose Marsh. Animal spotting and rewards are unchanged and your progress is kept.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (new checks: every section is fenced off, doorways and paths are open, the skywalk decks are solid, and a test walker climbs a ramp onto the skywalk). Not yet checked on a phone for speed.
