# Harbor City 0.34.7: towers, bridge road, bus shelters and City Loop buses

Includes everything from 0.34.6 (built but not released on its own):

- **Futuristic towers**: the old city's brick-and-block buildings are now glass towers on pearl bases: round towers with neon rings, stepped towers, twisting towers and slim towers with a floating sky garden.
- **Road onto the Marina bridge**: the avenue now runs straight over the promenade and beach onto the bridge. There used to be a strip of water you couldn't walk across. The Bridge Gate site moved a few metres to the side of the new road.
- **Modern bus stops**: every Airport Line stop is a glass shelter with a bench, a neon-edged roof and a digital screen that counts down to the next bus (and says BUS HERE when it arrives).
- **More buses**: six City Loop buses (violet, teal and pink) drive in traffic around downtown, the Civic Center and the highway. The Airport Line bus is still the one you ride.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed, including walking onto the bridge, the bus screens and buses stopping at red lights. About 52 fps on desktop.
