# Harbor City 0.41.0: the Sky Garden

- **Sky Garden** (Travel > Sky Garden): a raised garden terrace beside Harbor Galleria with eight beds. Buy exotic seeds at the Seed Stall, plant them and come back: they grow in real time, even while the game is closed. 12 plants from Common to Mythic (Sunburst Daisy 2 min ... Phoenix Palm 24 h). The stall restocks every 20 minutes (Uncommon 70%, Rare 40%, Epic 18%, Legendary 6%, Mythic 2% per restock). Mutations: Giant 8% (2x), Golden 6% (3x), Rainbow 1% (10x). Harvest for cash and XP, fill the Garden Codex, and become a Master Gardener (+$5,000). Plants never wither. Three free daisy seeds to start.
- **No cars in people places**: bollards at the Biosphere entrance, both Galleria doors and the Sky Garden ramp stop cars (people walk through). CALL CAR is blocked inside the Biosphere, the mall and the garden, and a car left inside is sent back outside.
- **Harbor Galleria**: calmer colours and five more shops: Sneaker Lab, Neon Outfitters, Pet Palace, Seed & Bloom and Harbor Sweets.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.
