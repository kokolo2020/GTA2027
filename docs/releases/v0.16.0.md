# Harbor City 0.16.0 — Skyline District

- **Skyline District**: a big new downtown west of the North Highway (about 335 x 380 m, bigger than the North Highway area). Enter through the two openings on the North Highway's west side, or take the new link road straight up from Bay Road. It opens together with Bay Road.
- Around 30 skyscrapers in five styles, including the 90 m Harbor Tower, plus Police HQ, City Hall, Grand Mall, Times Plaza with billboards, Central Park with a fountain and pond, Harbor Stadium and Union Station with a train.
- 8 new discovery sites (+$4,000 bonus for all 8), a new time trial (Skyline Dash, $1,200) and two new missions (Skyline Explorer, Rush Hour).
- **Traffic**: 22 AI cars drive on the right in the Skyline District and the old city. They stop at red lights (the signals really change), and for your car, people, the bus and police cars.
- **Busier old city**: sidewalks, crosswalks and traffic lights at every junction, new shop rows, benches, bins, hydrants, planters and rooftop billboards. Real palm trees on Bay Road and the beach.
- **More people**: 62 pedestrians (was 16), walking all over the Skyline District and standing at the plaza, park and station.
- Trials menu now has two columns and the journal's missions are split over two pages, so everything fits on screen.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; money, cars and progress are kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (district roads all drivable, pedestrian paths clear, sites pay once, traffic stops at red lights and for your car, old saves load). Not yet tested on the Fold5: performance in the Skyline District and with traffic, Skyline Dash difficulty.
