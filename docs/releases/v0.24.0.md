# Harbor City 0.24.0: Quick quests, 20 levels, every town open, starter crate, new sky and a Scan to play QR code

- **Quick quests** (new default activity): short pick-ups that pay on the spot. Go to the golden ring, tap **PICK UP**, get cash plus 50 XP, and the next quest starts straight away. The first one is right by the start. There are 20 in a chain: nine short walks around the old city, then a tour of every town. Menu now switches Quick quests → Delivery jobs → Exploring.
- **20 levels** instead of 5, quick to climb early (level 2 at 100 XP, level 5 at 700, level 20 at 10,450). Quick quests give 50 XP, every delivery job gives 25 XP. Your existing progress converts automatically.
- **Every town open from the start**: Ridge, Bay, North Highway, Skyline downtown and Marina Heights. No gates, and all are in the Travel list.
- **Starter crate**: new players pick one of three crates. It bursts open and reveals a car (Common Mint Cruiser, Rare Coast Runner or Epic Night Comet), a random paint job and $1,000 plus a $100–500 bonus, then DRIVE IT! starts you in the car. Existing players get one free Welcome back crate (no $1,000 starter money; +$500 if you already own that car).
- **Scan to play QR code** under the job panel: opens the browser version and turns Online on. Tap it to show it big. Sized to scan from a 720p-or-better livestream.
- **New look**: blue gradient sky with a sun, soft distance haze, richer colours, stronger sunlight with softer shadows, and subtle texture on roads, grass and pavements.
- **Walking arrow**: in the Follow view on foot, the direction arrow is now on screen at the top centre with the distance.
- **Fixed**: pine trees standing on the ends of the North Highway side streets; trees keep their original width (still taller).

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed:
- quick quest chain, pay and XP, mode switching
- 20 levels and all towns open
- starter crate (new and existing players)
- QR code (decoded from game screenshots, also after stream-like compression at 75% size)
- all earlier checks

Not yet tested on the Fold5 or in the browser, including Online switching on from the QR link.
