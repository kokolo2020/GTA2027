# Harbor City 0.47.0: tidy menu, wide pavements, open street stalls

- **New menu**: your name, money and level at the top, five tabs (GO, PLAY, ME, FRIENDS, SETTINGS) with big tiles, and one big Continue button.
- **Wider pavements downtown**: the downtown roads are 15 m instead of 20 m (still four lanes, a bit narrower), so the pavements are 7.5 m wide. Crossings, traffic lanes, signals, lamps, trees and people all moved to match.
- **Open street stalls**: the stadium food trucks are now open stalls (Hot Dog HQ with a grill and buns, Fresh Drinks with a bottle fridge and juice, the Fan Store with scarves and caps, Daily News, a Flower Cart and Gelato 21), and 17 more stalls line the downtown pavements. Each one shows its goods and has someone serving.
- **Fix**: some scenery (stall walls, parts of the lofts and the park) could fade out depending on where you stood; it now always shows.

Install over your existing app; do not uninstall. Same signing certificate; progress is kept. Debug-signed test build.

Desktop smoke test passed (traffic lights and lanes on the narrower roads, pedestrian paths clear, stalls off the road). Not yet checked on a phone. The North Highway and Airport Road keep their 20 m width.
